*The Ventana Pathway anti-HER2/neu (4B5) is a semi-quantitative
immunohistochemical assay to determine HER2/neu over-expression in
breast cancer tissues routinely processed for histologic evaluation.
This test has been approved by the FDA as an aid in the assessment of
patients for whom trastuzumab (Herceptin) treatment is being
considered. The kit used in this staining has been FDA approved for
breast and gastric cancers. As with any IHC type assay, a false
negative result is possible. A negative result does not exclude the
possibility of HER2/neu over-expression and clinically warranted,
more sensitive DNA based methodology (i.e. FISH) for evaluating gene
amplification may be indicated.

This assay is performed in concordance with CAP/ASCO criteria. The
results of this test are altered/affected by variables beyond our
laboratory's control, e.g. fixatives other than formalin and fixation
times other than those recommended in the CAP/ASCO guidelines. The
reporting ranges have been modified from the original FDA approved
reporting criteria to reflect those recommended in the updated
CAP/ASCO guidelines. This test is not intended as a confirmation of
the original diagnosis and should be used in conjunction with other
established risk factors, clinical and diagnostic procedures.

Patients with breast cancers that are HER2 IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH
amplified may be eligible for several therapies in that category HER2
signaling positive invasive breast cancers that test "HER2-Negative"
(IHC 0, 1+ or 2+/ISH not-amplified) are more specifically considered
"HER2-negative for protein overexpression/gene amplification" since
non-overexpressed levels of the HER2 protein may be present in these
cases. Patients with breast cancers that are HER2 IHC 0 (incomplete
faint membrane staining within <10% of the tumor cells), 1+, or
IHC 2+/ISH not amplified may be eligible for a treatment that targets
non-amplified/non-overexpressed levels of HER2 expression for
cytotoxic drug delivery.
