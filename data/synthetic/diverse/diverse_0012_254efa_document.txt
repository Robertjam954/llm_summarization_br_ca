MAMMOGRAM
Date of Examination: 08/05/2024
Technique: Standard bilateral two-view digital mammography (MLO and CC projections) with additional right ML and magnification views.

CLINICAL INDICATION: 58-year-old female presenting for annual screening mammogram. No palpable mass. Family history of breast cancer (mother, paternal aunt).

FINDINGS:

Right breast: There is an extensive cluster of pleomorphic and fine linear-branching calcifications spanning the upper outer quadrant of the right breast, centered at approximately the 10 o'clock position, approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple. The calcifications extend over a region measuring approximately 5.2 cm in greatest linear dimension, distributed in a segmental pattern. No associated soft tissue mass is identified. No architectural distortion is seen.

Left breast: The breast parenchyma demonstrates heterogeneous fibroglandular tissue. No suspicious mass, calcification, or architectural distortion is identified.

Lymph nodes: No pathologically enlarged axillary lymph nodes are seen bilaterally on the MLO views.

IMPRESSION:
Right breast: Segmental cluster of pleomorphic and fine linear-branching calcifications measuring approximately 5.2 cm in the right upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position, 4.5 cm from the nipple. These calcifications are highly suspicious for malignancy.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 5 — Highly Suggestive of Malignancy

RECOMMENDATION: Tissue sampling is recommended. Stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy is advised given the extent and morphology of the calcifications. MRI of the bilateral breasts may be obtained for further extent-of-disease evaluation prior to surgical planning.

MRI BILATERAL BREASTS
Date of Examination: 08/12/2024
Technique: Bilateral breast MRI performed on a 3.0 Tesla magnet with dedicated breast coil. Dynamic contrast- enhanced sequences obtained following intravenous administration of gadolinium-based contrast agent (gadobutrol, 0.1 mmol/kg). Subtraction images, kinetic analysis, and DWI sequences were obtained.

CLINICAL INDICATION: Known right breast suspicious calcifications on mammogram (BI-RADS 5). Extent-of- disease evaluation prior to biopsy and surgical planning.

FINDINGS:

Right breast: There is an extensive region of non-mass enhancement (NME) in the right upper outer quadrant, demonstrating a segmental distribution, at the 10 o'clock position approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple. This NME measures approximately 5.5 cm in greatest linear dimension. The internal enhancement pattern is clumped. Kinetic analysis demonstrates rapid initial enhancement with plateau kinetics. No discrete enhancing mass is identified within or separate from this region. There is no nipple or skin involvement. The pectoralis muscle and chest wall are uninvolved.

Additionally, there is a focus of non-mass enhancement in the right upper outer quadrant at approximately the 11 o'clock position, 3.5 cm from the nipple, measuring 0.6 cm, separate from the primary region of NME. This focus demonstrates similar clumped internal enhancement and rapid plateau kinetics, raising concern for additional satellite involvement within the same breast.

Left breast: No suspicious enhancing mass or NME is identified. No background parenchymal enhancement is diffusely elevated.

Lymph nodes: No morphologically abnormal axillary or internal mammary lymph nodes are identified bilaterally. Right axillary nodes are present and appear normal in size and morphology with preserved fatty hila.

IMPRESSION:
1. Right breast: Extensive segmental non-mass enhancement in the right upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position, measuring 5.5 cm, highly suspicious for malignancy (BI-RADS 5). Findings correspond to mammographic calcifications.
2. Additional focus of suspicious non-mass enhancement at the 11 o'clock position of the right breast (0.6 cm), raising concern for multicentric or multifocal involvement in the right breast.
3. No suspicious enhancement in the left breast.
4. No pathologically enlarged axillary or internal mammary lymph nodes bilaterally.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 5 — Highly Suggestive of Malignancy

RECOMMENDATION: Proceed with stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy of the right breast calcifications as previously recommended. MRI-guided biopsy of the right breast 11 o'clock focus may be considered following stereotactic biopsy results. Surgical oncology evaluation is advised upon pathologic confirmation.

STEREOTACTIC BIOPSY REPORT
Date of Procedure: 08/19/2024
Procedure Performed By: Dr. S. Patel, MD
Assisting Technologist: R. Okonkwo, RT(R)(M)

CLINICAL INDICATION: Extensive pleomorphic and fine linear-branching calcifications of the right upper outer quadrant, BI-RADS 5, for tissue sampling.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was positioned prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The right breast was compressed and pre-fire scout and stereo-pair images were obtained to confirm targeting of the calcification cluster in the right upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position. Local anesthesia was achieved with 1% lidocaine with epinephrine. A 9-gauge vacuum-assisted biopsy device (Hologic Affirm, 9g VAB) was used via a lateral approach. A total of 12 core specimens were obtained. Post-fire images confirmed adequate sampling of the target calcifications. Specimen radiography confirmed calcifications in 10 of 12 cores. Hemostasis was achieved without complication.

CLIP PLACEMENT: A titanium biopsy marker clip (Hologic HydroMARK, ribbon-shaped) was deployed at the biopsy site within the right upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position. Post- deployment mammographic images confirmed satisfactory clip position at the target site.

Post-procedure mammogram: Clip present at the target site. No pneumothorax or hematoma identified.

Specimens labeled: "Right breast 10 o'clock stereotactic biopsy cores x12" submitted in formalin for histopathologic analysis.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin, labeled "Right breast 10 o'clock stereotactic biopsy," are twelve tan-white cylindrical core needle biopsy specimens ranging from 0.8 to 1.4 cm in length. Specimen radiograph demonstrated calcifications in the majority of cores. All cores are submitted entirely in six cassettes.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show breast core biopsy tissue with ducts expanded by a proliferation of markedly pleomorphic epithelial cells with high nuclear grade, prominent nucleoli, and numerous mitotic figures, including atypical forms. The involved ducts demonstrate a solid and comedo growth pattern with central necrosis and dystrophic calcification. The calcifications are coarse and psammomatous in distribution, associated with the intraductal proliferation.

The lesion is present in 9 of 12 cores. No invasive carcinoma is identified in any of the submitted material. No lymphovascular invasion is seen. The stroma shows mild periductal fibrosis and chronic inflammation.

HISTOLOGIC DIAGNOSIS:
Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS), high nuclear grade, solid and comedo subtypes, with extensive associated calcifications, right breast, 10 o'clock position. Estimated extent on biopsy cores: at least 5.0 cm based on imaging correlation. No invasive carcinoma identified.
