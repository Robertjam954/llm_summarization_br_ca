STEREOTACTIC BIOPSY
Exam Date: 03/21/2024
Performing Radiologist: Sandra L. Whitmore, MD
Assisting Technologist: P. Nguyen, RT(R)(M)

CLINICAL INDICATION:
New grouped amorphous calcifications, left breast, 10 o'clock, posterior
depth. BI-RADS 4B. Stereotactic-guided biopsy requested for tissue sampling.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was positioned prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The left
breast was compressed and the target calcification cluster was localized using
standard digital stereotactic imaging. Local anesthesia was achieved with
1% lidocaine with epinephrine infiltration. Using a vacuum-assisted biopsy
(VAB) device, a 9-gauge Mammotome probe was advanced into the left breast
under stereotactic guidance, targeting the 10 o'clock amorphous calcification
cluster at posterior depth, approximately 5.5 cm from the nipple.

A total of twelve (12) core specimens were obtained. Post-fire stereo pair
images confirmed appropriate probe positioning within the target calcification
cluster.

SPECIMEN RADIOGRAPH:
Specimen radiograph was performed and confirmed the presence of calcifications
within multiple core specimens. Calcifications retrieved were deemed
representative of the target cluster.

CLIP PLACEMENT:
A BioMarker titanium clip (oval configuration) was deployed at the biopsy
site under stereotactic guidance following specimen retrieval. Post-clip
deployment mammographic images (CC and ML projections) confirmed the clip
to be positioned within the left breast upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock
biopsy site. Clip placement was accurate and concordant with the target
calcification cluster.

COMPLICATIONS: None.

PATHOLOGY SUBMITTED: Left breast stereotactic biopsy cores — sent to
Department of Pathology, Northgate Medical Center.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin labeled "left breast 10 o'clock stereotactic biopsy" are
twelve cylindrical core biopsy fragments ranging from 0.7 cm to 1.4 cm in

submitted for processing in two cassettes (A and B).

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections demonstrate fibrous breast stroma containing ductal structures lined
by a proliferation of intermediate-grade neoplastic epithelial cells. The
neoplastic cells exhibit moderate nuclear pleomorphism, inconspicuous nucleoli,
and occasional mitotic figures. The involved ducts display a cribriform and
focally solid growth pattern. Necrosis is absent. Calcifications are present
within the involved ducts and are consistent with the amorphous calcifications
identified on imaging. The process appears localized to the sampled region with
no evidence of involvement beyond the biopsy area on available material.
No invasive carcinoma is identified. No lymphovascular invasion is seen.

PATHOLOGIC DIAGNOSIS:

— DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), INTERMEDIATE NUCLEAR GRADE,
  cribriform and focally solid pattern, with associated intraluminal
  amorphous-type calcifications.
  Extent within cores: approximately 1.8 cm (correlates with imaging extent).
  No invasive carcinoma identified.

MARGINS: Not assessable on core biopsy material.
