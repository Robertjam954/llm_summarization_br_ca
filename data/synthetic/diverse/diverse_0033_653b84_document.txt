Under continuous real-time ultrasound guidance, the patient was positioned

left axillary lymph node was targeted. Following local anesthesia with 1%
lidocaine, a 22-gauge fine needle aspiration was performed with 3 passes.
Material was expressed onto glass slides and into cytology medium for cell
block preparation.

appropriately positioned within the left breast primary mass (L1) at the
10 o'clock position, mid-depth. No hematoma or pneumothorax identified.
