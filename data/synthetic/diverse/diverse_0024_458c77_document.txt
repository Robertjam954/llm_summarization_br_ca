CLINICAL INDICATION:
Grouped amorphous calcifications, left breast, 10 o'clock position,
approximately 4.0 cm from the nipple, BI-RADS 4B on diagnostic mammography
dated 03/14/2024.

TECHNIQUE:
The patient was positioned prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The left breast
was compressed and the target calcification cluster was localized using digital
stereotactic imaging. Following administration of local anesthesia (1% lidocaine
with epinephrine), a 9-gauge vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB) device (Hologic Eviva)
was introduced via a lateral approach. A total of twelve (12) core specimens were
obtained. Representative specimens were submitted for specimen radiography, which
confirmed retrieval of the target calcifications. A biopsy clip (titanium, omega-
shaped marker) was deployed at the biopsy site and post-deployment mammographic
images confirmed satisfactory clip placement within the area of calcification
sampling in the left upper-outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position. Hemostasis
was achieved. The patient tolerated the procedure without complication.

POST-PROCEDURE MAMMOGRAM:
Post-biopsy mammographic images demonstrate the omega-shaped titanium clip marker
within the targeted calcification cluster in the left breast at the 10 o'clock
position, approximately 4.0 cm from the nipple. Clip placement is concordant with
the biopsy target.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show fibrofatty breast tissue with ducts containing an intraductal
proliferation of intermediate-grade epithelial cells arranged in a cribriform and
micropapillary pattern. The neoplastic cells display moderate nuclear pleomorphism,
with conspicuous nucleoli and occasional mitotic figures. Necrosis is focal and
punctate (comedonecrosis is not a predominant feature). Calcifications of the
amorphous type are identified within involved ducts, consistent with the imaging
findings. The process appears localized without evidence of multifocal or multicentric
involvement within the submitted cores. No invasive carcinoma is identified. No
lymphovascular invasion is seen.

HISTOLOGIC DIAGNOSIS:
Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS), intermediate nuclear grade, cribriform and
micropapillary subtypes; spanning approximately 1.6 cm based on the extent across
submitted cores; left breast, 10 o'clock position.
Amorphous-type calcifications present within DCIS-involved ducts.
No invasive carcinoma identified.
