BIOPSY PROCEDURE NOTE

Date of Procedure: 03/08/2024
Indication: Suspicious right breast mass, 10 o'clock position.
Operator: R. Castellano, M.D. (Breast Radiologist)

PROCEDURE:
The patient was positioned in the supine oblique position. The right breast was
prepped and draped in the usual sterile fashion. Local anesthesia was achieved with
1% lidocaine with epinephrine. Under real-time ultrasound guidance, a 14-gauge
automated spring-loaded core needle biopsy device was advanced into the right breast
mass at the 10 o'clock position, 4 cm from the nipple. Five core specimens were
obtained in satisfactory fashion and placed in formalin for histologic processing.

CLIP PLACEMENT: Following tissue sampling, a titanium BB marker clip was deployed
at the biopsy site under ultrasound guidance. Clip position was confirmed with
post-deployment mammographic orthogonal views (CC and MLO), demonstrating the BB
marker clip to be within the sampled mass in the right upper outer quadrant.
Post-procedure mammogram images are on file.

RIGHT AXILLARY LYMPH NODE: A separate ultrasound-guided fine-needle aspiration was
performed of the enlarged right low axillary lymph node using a 25-gauge needle. Two
passes were obtained and material was expressed onto glass slides for cytologic
evaluation and placed in CytoLyt for liquid-based preparation.

The patient tolerated the procedure well. Standard post-biopsy instructions were
provided. Surgical oncology referral placed.
