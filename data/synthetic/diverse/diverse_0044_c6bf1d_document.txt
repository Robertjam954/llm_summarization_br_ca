Procedure: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy (14-gauge, spring-loaded
device), right breast mass at 10 o'clock, 4.5 cm from the nipple.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
Written informed consent was obtained. The patient was placed in the
supine oblique position. The right breast was prepped and draped in
the usual sterile fashion. Local anesthesia was achieved with 1%
lidocaine without epinephrine, 5 mL, infiltrated along the anticipated

spring-loaded core biopsy needle was introduced via a lateral approach
into the right breast mass at the 10 o'clock position, 4.5 cm from the
nipple. A total of five (5) core samples were obtained and submitted
in formalin for histopathologic analysis.

was deployed through the biopsy needle under ultrasound guidance and
confirmed to be positioned within the center of the targeted lesion
at the 10 o'clock position, right breast. Post-deployment mammographic
images (CC and lateral views) confirmed accurate clip placement within
the biopsy site. No significant hematoma was noted.

A separate fine-needle aspiration (FNA) of the suspicious low right
axillary lymph node was performed under ultrasound guidance using a
22-gauge needle. Material was submitted for cytologic evaluation.

SPECIMENS SUBMITTED:
1. Right breast 10 o'clock core biopsies x5 (formalin)
