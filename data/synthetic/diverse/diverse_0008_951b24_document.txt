STEREOTACTIC BIOPSY PROCEDURE NOTE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Date of Procedure: 02/22/2024
Indication: Left breast grouped amorphous calcifications at 10 o'clock, BI-RADS 4B,
as described on diagnostic mammogram 02/14/2024. Informed consent obtained.

PROCEDURE: Stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB), 9-gauge, left breast.

TECHNIQUE: The patient was positioned prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The
left breast was compressed. Scout, stereo pair, and pre-fire images were obtained.
The target calcifications at the 10 o'clock position, anterior-to-mid depth, 3.5 cm
from the nipple were localized under stereotactic guidance. The skin was prepped
and draped in standard sterile fashion. Local anesthesia was administered with 1%
lidocaine with epinephrine. A small skin nick was made and a 9-gauge vacuum-assisted
needle was advanced to the target site. Multiple cores (12 passes) were obtained and
submitted in formalin-filled specimen containers labeled "left breast stereotactic
biopsy — 10 o'clock."

SPECIMEN RADIOGRAPH: A specimen radiograph was performed and confirmed retrieval of
representative calcifications within the core specimens.

CLIP PLACEMENT: A titanium biopsy marker clip (MicroMark II, oval-shaped) was deployed
at the biopsy site upon completion of sampling. Post-deployment mammographic images
(CC and lateral) confirmed accurate clip placement at the 10 o'clock position, anterior-
to-mid depth of the left breast, co-incident with the calcification cluster. No
significant hematoma or procedural complication was identified.

POST-PROCEDURE MAMMOGRAM: Post-biopsy two-view mammogram confirmed the marker clip
is in appropriate position within the targeted area. Residual calcifications may
remain; pathologic-radiologic correlation is recommended.

──────────────────────────────────────
MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION
──────────────────────────────────────
Sections demonstrate fibrofatty breast parenchyma with ductal structures showing
intraluminal proliferation. There is expansion of the ductal spaces by a monomorphic
population of intermediate-grade epithelial cells exhibiting mild-to-moderate nuclear
pleomorphism with occasional mitoses. The cells are arranged in a cribriform and
micropapillary architectural pattern. Necrosis is focal and not of the comedo type.
Calcifications are present within the involved ducts and are consistent with the
mammographically identified amorphous calcifications. No stromal invasion is identified
in the sampled tissue. No lymphovascular invasion is seen. The findings span an
estimated extent of 1.8 cm across the submitted core material.
