ULTRASOUND OF THE LEFT BREAST
Date of Examination: 02/14/2024
Referring Clinician: Dr. R. Vasquez, MD
Technologist: M. Hollis, RDMS
Interpreting Radiologist: Dr. S. Okafor, MD

CLINICAL INDICATION:
[PATIENT] is a 47-year-old female presenting with a palpable left breast mass
noted on clinical examination approximately two weeks prior. Patient reports no
prior breast biopsies. Family history is notable for maternal aunt with breast
cancer diagnosed at age 52.

TECHNIQUE:
Real-time grayscale and color Doppler ultrasound of the left breast was performed
using a high-frequency linear transducer (12–5 MHz). Representative images were
obtained in radial and anti-radial planes throughout all quadrants and the
retroareolar region.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
A large, irregular, hypoechoic mass was identified in the left breast upper outer
quadrant at the 10 o'clock position, approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple, at a
mid-depth level. The mass measured 3.8 x 3.1 x 2.9 cm in three orthogonal
dimensions. Margins are spiculated and angulated. There is marked internal
hypervascularity on color Doppler interrogation. Posterior acoustic features
include mild posterior shadowing. No internal calcifications were identified on
sonographic evaluation.

LEFT AXILLA:
An abnormal-appearing lymph node was identified in the left axilla measuring
1.4 x 0.9 cm with cortical thickening to 6 mm and loss of the normal fatty hilum,
raising concern for metastatic involvement.

RIGHT BREAST AND AXILLA:
Not evaluated in this examination.

IMPRESSION:
1. Left breast upper outer quadrant mass at 10 o'clock, 4.5 cm from the nipple,
   measuring 3.8 x 3.1 x 2.9 cm. Morphology is highly suspicious for primary
   malignancy. BI-RADS 5.
2. Abnormal left axillary lymph node with cortical thickening, suspicious for
   metastatic involvement.

RECOMMENDATION:
Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast mass is recommended.
Ultrasound-guided sampling of the abnormal left axillary lymph node should also
be considered for staging purposes. Correlation with MRI of the bilateral
breasts is recommended for extent-of-disease evaluation prior to surgical
planning.

ULTRASOUND-GUIDED CORE NEEDLE BIOPSY — LEFT BREAST
Date of Procedure: 02/16/2024
Performing Radiologist: Dr. S. Okafor, MD
Procedure Nurse: T. Malone, RN

INDICATION:
Left breast 10 o'clock mass, BI-RADS 5, for tissue diagnosis.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
Informed consent was obtained. The patient was placed in the supine-oblique
position. The target lesion in the left upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock
position was identified under real-time ultrasound guidance. The overlying skin
was prepped with chlorhexidine solution and infiltrated with 1% lidocaine for
local anesthesia. A 14-gauge automated core needle biopsy device was advanced
into the lesion under continuous ultrasound visualization. Five passes were
performed yielding adequate tan-white cores of tissue. Specimens were placed in
formalin and submitted to pathology.

NOTE REGARDING CLIP PLACEMENT:
No biopsy clip/marker was placed at the conclusion of this procedure. The
operator elected to defer clip placement given the large size of the lesion and
anticipation of pre-operative MRI for surgical planning. The lesion is
considered sonographically and clinically identifiable without a marker.

Post-procedure mammogram was not performed.

Hemostasis was achieved with manual compression. The patient tolerated the
procedure without complication and was discharged to the care of her escort.

MRI OF THE BILATERAL BREASTS
Date of Examination: 02/22/2024
Interpreting Radiologist: Dr. A. Nkemdirim, MD, FSBI
MRI Technologist: P. Gruber, RT(MR)

CLINICAL INDICATION:
Known left breast malignancy (biopsy-proven, see pathology dated 02/19/2024).
MRI requested for extent-of-disease evaluation and surgical planning.

TECHNIQUE:
Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla scanner (Siemens Skyra) using
a dedicated 18-channel bilateral breast coil. Protocol included: axial T1-weighted
non-fat-suppressed, axial T2-weighted fat-suppressed, and dynamic contrast-
enhanced (DCE) sequences acquired pre-contrast and at multiple post-contrast time
points (1.5 minutes, 3.0 minutes, 6.0 minutes) following intravenous
administration of 0.1 mmol/kg gadobutrol (Gadavist). Subtraction and maximum
intensity projection (MIP) images were reconstructed. Computer-aided detection
(CAD) analysis was performed.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
There is a large, irregularly marginated, heterogeneously enhancing mass in the
left breast upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position, mid-depth, located
approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple. The mass measures 4.1 x 3.5 x 3.2 cm on
post-contrast T1-weighted sequences (longest dimension 4.1 cm), and demonstrates
rapid initial enhancement followed by washout kinetics (Type III curve) on dynamic
analysis. The mass appears slightly larger than on antecedent ultrasound, likely
reflecting improved soft tissue contrast resolution on MRI.

In addition, there is an area of non-mass enhancement (NME) in the left breast
upper outer quadrant in close proximity to the primary mass, extending superolaterally
over approximately 2.3 cm in a clumped, heterogeneous distribution. This NME could
represent satellite involvement or in-situ component and should be considered for
surgical planning.

No additional separate masses or areas of enhancement are identified in the
left breast to suggest multifocal or multicentric disease beyond the primary mass
and adjacent NME.

T2-weighted images demonstrate a large T2-hypointense mass corresponding to the
enhancing lesion. No T2-bright signal to suggest a cystic or mucinous component.

No skin thickening or nipple-areolar complex involvement is identified.
No suspicious pectoralis muscle invasion.

LEFT AXILLA:
Enlarged left axillary lymph node identified measuring 1.6 x 1.0 cm with
eccentric cortical thickening. Appearance is suspicious for metastatic involvement,
consistent with the sonographic finding of 02/14/2024.

RIGHT BREAST AND AXILLA:
The right breast demonstrates scattered fibroglandular tissue. No suspicious
mass, abnormal enhancement, or lymphadenopathy identified in the right breast
or right axilla.

IMPRESSION:
1. Left breast upper outer quadrant mass at the 10 o'clock position, mid-depth,
   4.5 cm from the nipple, measuring 4.1 x 3.5 x 3.2 cm on MRI. Rapid wash-
   out enhancement kinetics. Consistent with known biopsy-proven malignancy.
   BI-RADS 6.
2. Adjacent non-mass enhancement (NME) in the left upper outer quadrant extending
   superolaterally, approximately 2.3 cm, clumped distribution. Consistent with
   satellite or in-situ component.
3. Suspicious left axillary lymphadenopathy.
4. No MRI evidence of multifocal or multicentric disease in the left breast beyond
   the primary mass and associated NME. Right breast unremarkable.

RECOMMENDATION:
Correlation with pathology results. Surgical oncology and multidisciplinary tumor
board evaluation recommended. Consider neoadjuvant chemotherapy planning given
lesion size and suspected nodal involvement. Axillary lymph node sampling
recommended if not yet performed.

SPECIMEN:
Left breast, upper outer quadrant — ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy
(14-gauge, 5 cores)

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin are five tan-white to tan-pink cylindrical cores of tissue,
each measuring approximately 1.5–1.8 cm in length and 0.1 cm in diameter.
All cores are submitted entirely in one cassette.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show cores of breast tissue with complete replacement of the normal
parenchymal architecture by a highly cellular invasive carcinoma. The neoplastic
cells are arranged in solid nests and sheets with focal single-file pattern.
Individual tumor cells are large and pleomorphic with vesicular nuclei, prominent
nucleoli, and moderate-to-abundant amphophilic cytoplasm. Mitotic figures are
numerous, exceeding 10 per 10 high-power fields. Central necrosis is present
within tumor nests. A brisk stromal and intratumoral lymphocytic infiltrate (TIL
score approximately 40%) is identified. No lymphovascular invasion is definitively
identified on core biopsy material. No normal ductal or lobular architecture is
preserved within the sampled cores.

HISTOLOGIC GRADE (modified Scarff-Bloom-Richardson/Nottingham):
- Tubule formation: Score 3 (< 10% tubules)
- Nuclear pleomorphism: Score 3 (marked variation)
- Mitotic count: Score 3 (> 10 mitoses per 10 HPF)
- Total Score: 9/9 — Grade 3 (Poorly differentiated)

DIAGNOSIS:
Left breast, upper outer quadrant, ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy:
INVASIVE DUCTAL CARCINOMA (INVASIVE CARCINOMA OF NO SPECIAL TYPE),
NOTTINGHAM GRADE 3 (9/9).

Tumor size by imaging: 3.8 cm (ultrasound); see MRI report for updated measurement.
