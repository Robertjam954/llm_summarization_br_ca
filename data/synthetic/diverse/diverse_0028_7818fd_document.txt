CLINICAL INDICATION: 54-year-old female presenting for diagnostic mammogram
following patient report of right breast tenderness. Personal history of
benign biopsy (left breast, 2019). No family history of breast cancer.

TECHNIQUE: Standard craniocaudal (CC) and mediolateral oblique (MLO) views
bilat eral, with additional right breast spot-magnification views of the upper
outer quadrant.

FINDINGS:

RIGHT BREAST: There is a segmental distribution of pleomorphic calcifications
spanning the upper outer quadrant of the right breast, centered at
approximately the 10 o'clock position, at a mid-depth location, estimated
4.0 cm from the nipple. The calcifications are numerous and demonstrate
heterogeneous morphology with fine linear and fine-linear branching (casting)
forms, highly suspicious for ductal origin. The overall extent of the
calcification cluster measures approximately 4.2 cm in greatest linear
dimension. No associated mass, architectural distortion, or skin thickening
is identified. No dominant mass is seen.

LEFT BREAST: Scattered fibroglandular tissue. No suspicious mass, calcification,
or architectural distortion.

AXILLAE: No pathologically enlarged lymph nodes are identified bilaterally.

BREAST COMPOSITION: Heterogeneously dense breast tissue (Category C), which
may lower the sensitivity of mammography.

IMPRESSION:
Right breast: Segmental cluster of pleomorphic calcifications with fine linear
and fine-linear branching forms, upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock position,
mid depth, 4.0 cm from nipple, spanning approximately 4.2 cm.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 5 — Highly Suggestive of Malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION: Tissue sampling is recommended. Stereotactic-guided vacuum-
assisted biopsy of the right breast calcifications is advised. MRI of the
bilateral breast is also recommended for further extent-of-disease evaluation
prior to definitive surgical planning.

STEREOTACTIC VACUUM-ASSISTED CORE BIOPSY — RIGHT BREAST
Procedure Date: 03/07/2024
================================================================================

CLINICAL INDICATION: Segmental cluster of pleomorphic calcifications, right
breast upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock, BI-RADS 5.

PROCEDURE: The patient was placed prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The
right breast was immobilized in compression. Digital stereotactic targeting was
performed. Using local anesthesia (1% lidocaine with epinephrine), a 9-gauge
vacuum-assisted stereotactic needle (Mammotome system) was advanced to the
target site within the calcification cluster. Multiple core specimens (12 cores)
were obtained from the upper outer quadrant of the right breast. Calcifications
were confirmed present within the specimen cores by specimen radiograph. Post-
biopsy hemostasis was achieved with manual compression.

CLIP PLACEMENT: A titanium biopsy marker clip (oval-shaped) was deployed at
the biopsy site within the calcification cluster, right breast upper outer
quadrant, 10 o'clock position. Post-deployment mammographic images confirmed
accurate clip placement within the region of calcifications.

SPECIMEN: Twelve core specimens submitted in formalin to Surgical Pathology.
Specimen radiograph confirmed calcifications within submitted cores.

COMPLICATIONS: None.

Received in formalin labeled "right breast stereotactic biopsy cores" are
twelve cylindrical core specimens ranging from 0.9 to 1.4 cm in length and

submitted tissue. All cores submitted entirely in four cassettes.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show breast core tissue with ductal structures filled and distended
by a proliferation of markedly atypical epithelial cells. The cells demonstrate
large, pleomorphic nuclei with coarse chromatin, prominent nucleoli, and
frequent mitotic figures. The growth pattern is predominantly solid with
central comedo-type necrosis and associated dystrophic calcifications, consistent
with the mammographically identified calcification cluster. The calcifications
are dystrophic and psammomatous in focally varying areas. The overall intra-
ductal process is high nuclear grade.

No invasive carcinoma is identified. No stromal invasion is seen in any of
the twelve core specimens. Lymphovascular invasion: Not identified.

DIAGNOSIS:
Right breast, upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock, stereotactic vacuum-assisted
biopsy:

  — DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), HIGH NUCLEAR GRADE, SOLID AND COMEDO
    GROWTH PATTERNS WITH CENTRAL NECROSIS.
    Extent on biopsy cores consistent with a large-segment process; correlates
    with 4.2 cm mammographic and 4.5 cm MRI extent.
    NO INVASIVE CARCINOMA IDENTIFIED.

MARGINS: Not assessable on core biopsy.
