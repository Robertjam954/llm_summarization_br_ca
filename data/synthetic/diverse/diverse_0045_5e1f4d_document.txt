ULTRASOUND — LEFT BREAST AND AXILLA
Date of Examination: 03/12/2024
Technologist: M. Briggs, RDMS
Interpreting Radiologist: Dr. C. Afolabi, MD

CLINICAL INDICATION: Palpable lump, left breast, upper outer quadrant. 54-year-old female with
family history of breast cancer (maternal aunt). No prior breast imaging on file.

TECHNIQUE: High-resolution grayscale and color Doppler ultrasound of the left breast and left
axilla was performed using a 12–5 MHz linear transducer.

FINDINGS:

PRIMARY LESION (L1):
A hypoechoic, irregular mass with angular margins and posterior acoustic shadowing was identified
in the left breast at the 10 o'clock position, approximately 3.5 cm from the nipple, at a
mid-depth location. The mass measures 2.8 cm x 2.1 cm x 1.9 cm (AP x TR x CC). Internal
vascularity was increased on Doppler interrogation.

SECONDARY LESION (L2):
A second, smaller hypoechoic irregular mass was identified in the left breast at the 9 o'clock
position, approximately 4.2 cm from the nipple, also at mid-depth. This lesion measures 0.9 cm
x 0.7 cm x 0.8 cm and demonstrates similar sonographic characteristics to the primary mass.
The two lesions appear to occupy separate quadrants of the upper outer and lateral left breast.
Multifocal disease cannot be excluded.

LEFT AXILLA:
Several lymph nodes were identified in the left axillary region. The largest measures 2.2 cm in
short axis and demonstrates markedly thickened cortex (cortical thickness up to 8 mm), loss of
fatty hilum, and increased vascularity. Findings are highly suspicious for nodal metastatic
involvement.

CALCIFICATIONS: Coarse, heterogeneous calcifications were identified in association with the
primary mass at the 10 o'clock position. No additional suspicious calcification clusters were
identified elsewhere in the imaged field.

IMPRESSION:
1. Left breast 10 o'clock position: Hypoechoic irregular mass, 2.8 cm, with associated
   coarse calcifications, highly suspicious for malignancy. BI-RADS 5.
2. Left breast 9 o'clock position: Second irregular hypoechoic mass, 0.9 cm, suspicious for
   synchronous focus of malignancy. BI-RADS 5.
3. Left axilla: Morphologically abnormal lymph node, 2.2 cm short axis, highly suspicious
   for nodal metastatic disease.

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast 10 o'clock mass and
the left breast 9 o'clock mass is recommended. Ultrasound-guided fine needle aspiration or
core needle biopsy of the suspicious left axillary lymph node is also recommended. Surgical
oncology referral advised following tissue diagnosis.

contrast. Dynamic contrast-enhanced sequences were obtained. Subtraction and MIP images were

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:

washout kinetics (type III curve) is demonstrated. Associated regional non-mass enhancement

approximately 4.5 cm, consistent with an extensive intraductal component (DCIS).

SECONDARY MASS (L2): A second enhancing irregular mass is confirmed at the 9 o'clock
position, lateral left breast, 4.2 cm from the nipple, measuring 1.0 cm. Type III enhancement
kinetics are also demonstrated.

ADDITIONAL NON-MASS ENHANCEMENT: Segmental non-mass enhancement is identified in the upper
outer quadrant of the left breast, extending between and around the two masses over a
span of approximately 5.0 cm craniocaudally, consistent with multi focal/extensive DCIS
involvement in addition to the invasive foci.

SKIN AND NIPPLE: No definitive skin thickening or nipple invasion. Pectoralis muscle
appears intact.

LEFT AXILLA: Enlarged morphologically abnormal lymph nodes are identified in the left axilla,
the largest measuring 2.3 cm in short axis with cortical thickening. Highly suspicious for
metastatic adenopathy, consistent with ultrasound findings.

RIGHT BREAST: No suspicious mass, non-mass enhancement, or axillary adenopathy. BI-RADS 1
for right breast.

IMPRESSION:
1. Left breast 10 o'clock: Irregular enhancing mass, 3.1 cm, with type III kinetics,

   BI-RADS 6 (biopsy-proven malignancy).
2. Left breast 9 o'clock: Enhancing mass, 1.0 cm, second suspicious focus. BI-RADS 5.
3. Segmental NME in upper outer left breast spanning ~5.0 cm, concerning for extensive
   DCIS component.
4. Morphologically abnormal left axillary lymphadenopathy, consistent with nodal metastases.
5. Multifocal disease in the left breast.

RECOMMENDATION: Correlation with pathology results from ultrasound-guided biopsy.
Surgical oncology and multidisciplinary tumor board discussion recommended for staging
and treatment planning.

Date of Procedure: 03/22/2024
Performing Radiologist: Dr. C. Afolabi, MD
Technologist Assist: K. Osman, RDMS

PROCEDURE NOTE:

Informed consent was obtained. The patient was placed in the supine oblique position. The left
breast and left axilla were prepped and draped in the usual sterile fashion. Local anesthesia
was administered with 1% lidocaine.

BIOPSY — PRIMARY MASS (L1, 10 o'clock):
Using real-time ultrasound guidance, a 14-gauge automated core needle biopsy device was
advanced into the left breast 10 o'clock mass. Five cores were obtained and submitted in
formalin. Following sampling, a biopsy marker clip (HydroMARK coil, titanium) was deployed
at the biopsy site and confirmed by post-procedure ultrasound to be within the target lesion
at the 10 o'clock position. Post-procedure mammogram was recommended for clip localization
confirmation.

BIOPSY — SECONDARY MASS (L2, 9 o'clock):
Using real-time ultrasound guidance, a 14-gauge automated core needle biopsy device was
advanced into the left breast 9 o'clock mass. Four cores were obtained and submitted
separately in formalin. A second biopsy marker clip (HydroMARK coil, titanium) was deployed
at the 9 o'clock biopsy site and confirmed by post-procedure ultrasound.

BIOPSY — LEFT AXILLARY LYMPH NODE:
Using real-time ultrasound guidance, a 22-gauge fine needle was used to perform fine needle
aspiration (FNA) of the largest morphologically abnormal left axillary lymph node. Three
passes were made. Material was submitted on slides for cytologic evaluation.

COMPLICATIONS: None. Patient tolerated procedure well.

POST-PROCEDURE RECOMMENDATION: Post-procedure mammogram for clip localization. Pathology
follow-up. Surgical oncology consultation.
