ULTRASOUND — LEFT BREAST
Date of Examination: 02/06/2024
Indication: Palpable left breast mass. Patient presents with a firm,
fixed mass in the upper outer left breast noted for approximately
3 weeks. No prior breast imaging on file.

TECHNIQUE: High-resolution grayscale and Doppler ultrasound of the
left breast and left axilla was performed using a 12–5 MHz linear
array transducer.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST: A large, hypoechoic, irregular mass with angular
margins and posterior acoustic shadowing was identified in the
left breast upper outer quadrant (UOQ), 10 o'clock position,
approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple, at a mid-depth location
within the breast parenchyma. The mass measures 3.8 x 3.1 x 3.4 cm
in the transverse, anteroposterior, and craniocaudal dimensions,
respectively. Increased internal vascularity is noted on Doppler
imaging. No shadowing calcifications are identified sonographically.
There is no associated skin thickening or nipple retraction.

LEFT AXILLA: An enlarged lymph node measuring 2.0 x 1.4 cm is
identified in the left axilla with a thickened cortex (cortical
thickness 7 mm) and effaced fatty hilum, suspicious for metastatic
involvement.

RIGHT BREAST AND RIGHT AXILLA: Not imaged at this examination.

No biopsy clip or marker was placed at the time of this examination.

IMPRESSION:
1. 3.8 cm irregular hypoechoic mass, left breast UOQ, 10 o'clock
   position, mid depth, 4.5 cm from nipple. Highly suspicious for
   malignancy.
2. Enlarged, morphologically abnormal left axillary lymph node
   suspicious for metastatic involvement.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 5 — Highly Suggestive of Malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left
breast mass is recommended. Correlation with MRI for locoregional
staging is advised.

procedure. Hemostasis was achieved with manual compression.

MRI — BILATERAL BREASTS WITH AND WITHOUT CONTRAST
Date of Examination: 02/13/2024
Indication: Known left breast malignancy on core biopsy. Locoregional
staging. Patient M.T., age 52.

TECHNIQUE: Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla MRI
system using a dedicated 16-channel breast coil. Sequences included
axial T1-weighted, T2-weighted fat-suppressed, and dynamic
contrast-enhanced (DCE) series following intravenous administration
of gadolinium-based contrast agent (Gadobutrol 0.1 mmol/kg). DWI
and subtraction sequences were obtained. Kinetic curve analysis
was performed.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST — PRIMARY LESION: An irregular, spiculated enhancing
mass is identified in the left breast upper outer quadrant at the
10 o'clock position, mid depth, approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple.
The mass demonstrates heterogeneous early rapid enhancement with
washout kinetics on delayed phase imaging (Type III curve), consistent
with a malignant pattern. On DCE imaging, the mass measures 3.9 x
3.3 x 3.6 cm (transverse x AP x CC). The mass demonstrates restricted
diffusion on DWI (ADC value approximately 0.78 x 10^-3 mm²/s).

ADDITIONAL ENHANCEMENT — LEFT BREAST: Non-mass enhancement (NME)
of clumped, segmental distribution is identified extending from the
posterior margin of the primary mass toward the nipple along the
upper outer quadrant, spanning approximately 3.1 cm in greatest
dimension. This NME pattern is suspicious and raises concern for
associated ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS) or satellite disease.
No separate discrete secondary mass is identified.

LEFT AXILLA: At least one morphologically abnormal lymph node is
identified in the left axilla with a thickened cortex and loss
of fatty hilum, measuring up to 2.1 cm in short axis, consistent
with the ultrasound findings and highly suspicious for metastatic
nodal involvement.

RIGHT BREAST: No suspicious enhancing mass, non-mass enhancement,
or axillary adenopathy identified in the right breast.

SKIN AND CHEST WALL: No skin involvement or chest wall invasion.
No pectoralis muscle involvement.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 6 — Known biopsy-proven malignancy (left breast).
Left axillary adenopathy suspicious for nodal metastasis.

RECOMMENDATION: Findings were reviewed with the surgical oncology
team. Recommend multidisciplinary tumor board discussion. No
additional biopsy recommended at this time pending pathology
correlation. Axillary lymph node sampling recommended at time of
surgery or by image-guided biopsy per surgical oncology preference.
