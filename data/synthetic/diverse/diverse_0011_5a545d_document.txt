MRI — BREAST (BILATERAL, WITH AND WITHOUT CONTRAST)
Date of Exam: 03/11/2024
Indication: Pre-operative staging for right breast suspicious calcifications (BI-RADS 5 on mammogram). Evaluate extent of disease.

TECHNIQUE:
Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla scanner using a dedicated breast coil. Pre- and post-gadolinium sequences were acquired including T1-weighted fat-suppressed dynamic contrast-enhanced (DCE) series, T2-weighted, and diffusion-weighted sequences. Gadolinium-based contrast agent was administered intravenously.

FINDINGS:

RIGHT BREAST:
There is an extensive region of clumped non-mass enhancement (NME) in segmental distribution throughout the right breast upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position. The NME measures approximately 5.8 cm in craniocaudal extent and extends from 2 cm to 7 cm from the right nipple at a mid-to-posterior depth. Kinetic analysis of the enhancing region demonstrates predominantly plateau kinetics with focal areas of washout. No discrete enhancing mass is identified within or adjacent to the region of NME. No skin involvement or pectoral muscle enhancement.

In addition to the primary area of NME, there is a secondary focus of non-mass enhancement in the right breast upper inner quadrant at the 12 o'clock position, measuring approximately 0.7 cm, located approximately 3.5 cm from the nipple. This focus demonstrates similar kinetic characteristics and is suspicious for additional involvement.

LEFT BREAST:
No suspicious mass, non-mass enhancement, or architectural distortion. No abnormal lymph nodes visualized in the left axilla.

LYMPH NODES:
Two right axillary lymph nodes are identified, both with preserved fatty hila and measuring up to 1.1 cm in short axis. No morphologically abnormal lymph nodes are identified in the right axilla. No left axillary lymphadenopathy.

IMPRESSION:
1. Extensive segmental non-mass enhancement in the right breast upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position measuring approximately 5.8 cm, consistent with the known extensive calcifications and highly suspicious for high-grade DCIS. No associated discrete enhancing mass to suggest invasive component.
2. Additional suspicious non-mass enhancement focus in the right breast upper inner quadrant at the 12 o'clock position measuring 0.7 cm, raising concern for multicentric disease. MRI-guided biopsy or correlation with stereotactic findings advised.
3. No pathologically enlarged axillary lymph nodes bilaterally.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 6 (pending tissue diagnosis from stereotactic biopsy; additional focus assigned BI-RADS 4B)

RECOMMENDATION: Proceed with planned stereotactic biopsy. Surgical consultation for pre-operative planning given extent of right breast involvement and possible multicentric disease. Consider MRI-guided biopsy of the secondary 12 o'clock focus.

PROCEDURE NOTE:
The patient was placed prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The right breast was compressed and the calcification cluster at the 10 o'clock position was localized using stereotactic guidance. After sterile preparation and administration of 1% lidocaine local anesthesia, an 8-gauge vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB) needle was introduced via a lateral approach to the right breast. Multiple core specimens (12 cores) were obtained from representative areas of the calcification cluster. All specimens were submitted in formalin for pathologic analysis.

A titanium biopsy marker clip (ribbon configuration) was deployed at the biopsy site within the right breast upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock position, approximately 4 cm from the nipple. Post-deployment mammographic views confirmed satisfactory clip placement at the target site.

COMPLICATIONS: None.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show multiple cores of fibrofatty breast tissue with ductal structures. There is extensive involvement of the ducts by high-grade ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS). The DCIS demonstrates a predominantly solid and cribriform growth pattern with central necrosis (comedonecrosis). Nuclear grade is high (grade 3), with markedly enlarged nuclei, coarse chromatin, and prominent nucleoli. Mitotic figures are readily identified. Extensive microcalcifications are present within and adjacent to the involved ducts, corresponding to the mammographic and MRI findings. No invasive carcinoma is identified in the submitted material. No lymphovascular invasion is seen. The involved ducts encompass the majority of the submitted core material.

DIAGNOSIS:
Right breast, 10 o'clock position, stereotactic vacuum-assisted core biopsy:
— DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), HIGH-GRADE (nuclear grade 3), solid and cribriform pattern with comedonecrosis, with extensive associated microcalcifications. No invasive carcinoma identified.

COMMENT: Findings are consistent with high-grade DCIS with extensive calcifications. Extent of disease on imaging (mammogram 5.5 cm, MRI NME 5.8 cm) suggests wide excision or mastectomy will be required. Clinical and multidisciplinary correlation recommended.
