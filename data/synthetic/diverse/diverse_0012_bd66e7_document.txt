of NME.

enhancement.

noted throughout the right breast, consistent with background parenchymal
enhancement (BPE) — mild, symmetric.

Performing Radiologist: Dr. S. Mehta, MD

INDICATION:
Extensive segmental pleomorphic and fine linear branching calcifications,
right breast upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock position, BI-RADS 5.
Biopsy requested for tissue diagnosis.

TECHNIQUE:
Informed consent was obtained. The patient was positioned prone on the
stereotactic biopsy table. The right breast was compressed and stereo pair
images were obtained to localize the target calcifications. Local anesthesia
was achieved with 1% lidocaine with epinephrine. A 9-gauge vacuum-assisted
biopsy (VAB) device was deployed using stereotactic guidance. Twelve cores
were obtained from the target calcification cluster in the right breast upper
outer quadrant. A specimen radiograph was performed, confirming the presence
of representative calcifications in multiple cores.

A titanium biopsy clip marker (MicroMark II, elliptical shape) was deployed
at the biopsy site within the right upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock position,
approximately 3.0 cm from the nipple, under stereotactic guidance. Post-
deployment mammographic images (CC and lateral) confirmed satisfactory clip
placement at the target site with no significant migration.

POST-PROCEDURE FINDINGS:
Small hematoma at the biopsy site. No pneumothorax. Patient tolerated the
procedure well. Standard post-procedure care instructions provided.

SPECIMENS:
Twelve cores submitted in formalin, labeled "Right breast 10 o'clock,
stereotactic VAB." Specimen radiograph demonstrates calcifications within
the cores.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin are twelve tan-white core needle biopsy fragments
ranging from 0.8 to 1.4 cm in length and 0.1 to 0.2 cm in diameter. The
specimen radiograph submitted with the specimen confirms the presence of
calcifications within the tissue fragments. All cores are entirely submitted
in seven cassettes.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show fibrofatty breast parenchyma with multiple ducts involved by a
high-grade intraductal proliferation. The neoplastic cells are markedly
pleomorphic with prominent nucleoli, coarse chromatin, and frequent mitotic
figures (nuclear grade 3). The pattern of growth is predominantly solid with
focal comedo-type central necrosis. Extensive calcifications are identified
in association with the necrotic areas and within the ductal lumens, spanning
multiple core fragments and consistent with the segmental distribution
observed radiologically.

No invasive carcinoma is identified in the sampled tissue. No lymphovascular
invasion is seen. No normal epithelium is present outside of foci involved by
the high-grade intraductal process.

The calcifications are present in six of the twelve submitted cores.

DIAGNOSIS:
Right breast, 10 o'clock position, stereotactic vacuum-assisted core biopsy:
DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), HIGH GRADE, solid and comedo type,
with extensive associated calcifications.
— Estimated extent across biopsy: spanning approximately 5.5 cm based on
  radiologic-pathologic correlation.
— Nuclear grade: 3 (high grade).
— Necrosis: Present, comedo-type.
— No invasive carcinoma identified.

MARGINS: Not applicable (core needle biopsy specimen).
