TECHNIQUE:
High-resolution grayscale and Doppler ultrasound of the left breast and left axilla was
performed using a 12–5 MHz linear-array transducer. Multiple axial and sagittal images
were obtained in a radial and anti-radial fashion.

FINDINGS:

PRIMARY LESION (Index — L1):
At the 9 o'clock position of the left breast, approximately 3.5 cm from the nipple,
at a mid to posterior depth, an irregularly shaped hypoechoic mass was identified
measuring 2.8 cm x 2.1 cm x 1.9 cm (AP x transverse x craniocaudal). The margins
were spiculated and angulated. Posterior acoustic shadowing was noted. Internal
vascularity was present on color Doppler interrogation.

SECONDARY LESION (L2):
A second, smaller hypoechoic mass was identified at the 8 o'clock position of the left
breast, approximately 4.2 cm from the nipple, at a mid depth, measuring 1.1 cm x
0.9 cm x 0.8 cm. Margins were indistinct. This lesion was spatially separate from the
index mass but within the same quadrant (outer upper quadrant/lower outer quadrant
transition zone).

CALCIFICATIONS:
Punctate echogenic foci consistent with microcalcifications were identified within and
immediately adjacent to the index mass (L1). No acoustic shadowing was associated with
these foci.

LEFT AXILLA:
An abnormal left axillary lymph node was identified measuring 2.3 cm in greatest
dimension. The node demonstrated markedly thickened cortex (cortical thickness > 4 mm,
measuring 7 mm) with loss of the fatty hilum, highly suspicious for metastatic
involvement.

IMPRESSION:
1. Irregular spiculated hypoechoic mass, left breast, 9 o'clock, 3.5 cm from nipple,
   measuring 2.8 cm — highly suspicious for malignancy.
2. Second spatially distinct suspicious hypoechoic mass, left breast, 8 o'clock,
   4.2 cm from nipple, measuring 1.1 cm — suspicious for malignancy; multifocal
   disease cannot be excluded.
3. Microcalcifications associated with the index mass.
4. Abnormal left axillary lymph node, morphologically suspicious for metastatic disease.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 5 — Highly suggestive of malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast index mass
(L1) and the secondary lesion (L2) is strongly recommended. Ultrasound-guided biopsy
of the abnormal left axillary lymph node is also recommended. MRI of the bilateral
breasts is recommended for extent-of-disease evaluation prior to surgical planning.

TECHNIQUE:
Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla magnet with a dedicated breast coil.
Sequences included T1-weighted, T2-weighted fat-saturated, and dynamic contrast-enhanced
(DCE) series utilizing 0.1 mmol/kg gadobutrol administered intravenously. Subtraction
and maximum intensity projection (MIP) images were generated. CAD analysis was applied.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST — INDEX MASS (L1):
At the 9 o'clock position, mid to posterior depth, approximately 3.5 cm from the
nipple, an irregular mass with spiculated margins demonstrated rapid initial enhancement
with washout kinetics (type III curve), measuring 2.9 cm x 2.2 cm x 2.0 cm on
contrast-enhanced sequences. This corresponds to the sonographic index lesion. The
enhancement pattern and kinetics are highly suspicious for invasive carcinoma.

LEFT BREAST — SECONDARY FOCUS (L2):
A second enhancing mass at the 8 o'clock position, mid depth, 4.2 cm from the nipple,
measuring 1.2 cm x 1.0 cm x 0.9 cm, demonstrates similar rapid enhancement and
washout kinetics. This focus is spatially separate from L1, confirming multifocal
disease in the left breast.

NON-MASS ENHANCEMENT (NME):
Clumped non-mass enhancement (NME) was identified in the upper outer quadrant of the
left breast, spanning approximately 4.5 cm in craniocaudal extent, bridging the region
between L1 and L2 and extending superiorly. The distribution is segmental. This
pattern is suspicious and may represent an extensive intraductal (DCIS) component
associated with the invasive disease.

RIGHT BREAST:
No suspicious mass, non-mass enhancement, or architectural distortion identified in
the right breast. Background parenchymal enhancement is mild and symmetric.

LEFT AXILLA:
The previously identified abnormal left axillary lymph node measures 2.4 cm on MRI.
Cortical thickening and loss of fatty hilum are again demonstrated. No additional
pathological lymph nodes were identified in the right axilla.

SKIN / CHEST WALL:
No skin thickening, nipple retraction, or chest wall involvement identified.

IMPRESSION:
1. Dominant irregular enhancing mass, left breast, 9 o'clock, 3.5 cm from the nipple,
   2.9 cm — highly suspicious for invasive carcinoma (BI-RADS 6, biopsy-proven at time
   of MRI dictation pending).
2. Second spatially separate enhancing mass, left breast, 8 o'clock, 4.2 cm from
   nipple, 1.2 cm — confirming multifocal left breast disease.
3. Segmental clumped non-mass enhancement spanning approximately 4.5 cm in the upper
   outer quadrant of the left breast, suspicious for extensive DCIS component.
4. Abnormal left axillary lymph node, 2.4 cm, compatible with metastatic involvement.
5. No suspicious findings in the right breast.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 6 — Known biopsy-proven malignancy (index lesion). Additional
findings require pathologic correlation and/or biopsy.

RECOMMENDATION: Correlation with biopsy results; multidisciplinary tumor board
review recommended. Surgical oncology consultation for operative planning given
multifocal disease and axillary involvement. Consider MRI-guided biopsy of the
non-mass enhancement if not adequately sampled by ultrasound-guided biopsy.

PROCEDURE NOTE:

Informed consent was obtained. The patient was positioned supine with the left arm
abducted. The left breast and axilla were prepped and draped in the usual sterile
fashion. Local anesthesia was achieved with 1% lidocaine without epinephrine.

LEFT BREAST — INDEX LESION (L1):
Under continuous real-time ultrasound guidance, a 14-gauge automated core biopsy gun
(Bard Magnum) was introduced and directed to the left breast mass at the 9 o'clock
position, 3.5 cm from the nipple. Five (5) cores were obtained and placed in formalin.
Following sampling, a radiopaque biopsy clip (titanium omega-shaped marker, Cormark
system) was deployed at the biopsy site. Post-deployment mammographic confirmation
images (craniocaudal and mediolateral oblique views) confirmed accurate clip placement
within the left breast at the 9 o'clock lesion site.

LEFT BREAST — SECONDARY LESION (L2):
Under continuous real-time ultrasound guidance, a 14-gauge automated core biopsy gun
was introduced and directed to the second left breast mass at the 8 o'clock position,
4.2 cm from the nipple. Four (4) cores were obtained and placed in formalin. A second
radiopaque biopsy clip (titanium omega-shaped marker) was deployed at this biopsy site.
Post-deployment images confirmed accurate placement.

LEFT AXILLARY LYMPH NODE:
Under ultrasound guidance, a 22-gauge fine needle aspiration (FNA) was performed on
the abnormal left axillary lymph node. Material was expressed onto slides and placed
in CytoLyt transport medium.

COMPLICATIONS: None. Patient tolerated the procedure well. Post-procedural care
instructions provided.
