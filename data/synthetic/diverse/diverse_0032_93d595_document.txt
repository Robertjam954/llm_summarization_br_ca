MRI — BILATERAL BREASTS WITH AND WITHOUT CONTRAST
DATE: 03/11/2024
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CLINICAL INDICATION: Known left breast mass, BI-RADS 5 on ultrasound. MRI requested
for extent of disease evaluation prior to surgical planning.

TECHNIQUE: Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla scanner using a dedicated
breast coil. Pre- and post-contrast sequences were obtained following intravenous
administration of gadolinium-based contrast agent (Gadobutrol, 0.1 mmol/kg).
Subtraction and dynamic kinetic curve analysis were performed.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
An irregularly shaped, heterogeneously enhancing mass is identified in the left breast
at the 10 o'clock position, posterior depth, approximately 4.3 cm from the nipple. The
mass measures 4.1 x 3.6 x 3.4 cm on MRI (CC x AP x ML). Margins are irregular and
spiculated. Dynamic kinetic curve analysis demonstrates initial rapid enhancement
with washout kinetics (type III curve), consistent with high suspicion for malignancy.
There is associated skin thickening overlying the mass measuring up to 4 mm. No
nipple-areolar complex involvement is identified.

In addition to the primary mass, there is a region of non-mass enhancement (NME)
in the left breast at the 9 o'clock position, anterior depth, spanning approximately
2.1 cm in segmental distribution, located approximately 2.8 cm from the nipple. This
NME is separate from and not contiguous with the primary mass, raising concern for an
additional site of disease within the same breast.

No enhancement is identified in the right breast. No abnormal enhancement is identified
in the right axilla.

LEFT AXILLA: Two enlarged lymph nodes are identified in the left axilla with cortical
thickening and loss of normal fatty hilum, consistent with the ultrasound findings
and suspicious for metastatic involvement.

CHEST WALL: Pectoralis muscle does not appear involved.

IMPRESSION:
1. Large irregularly enhancing mass, left breast, 10 o'clock position, posterior depth,
   4.1 cm (greatest dimension), approximately 4.3 cm from the nipple. Type III kinetic
   curve. BI-RADS 6 (known biopsy-proven or highly suspicious pending biopsy —
   noted pre-biopsy MRI).
2. Separate focus of non-mass enhancement (NME), left breast, 9 o'clock, anterior depth,
   spanning 2.1 cm, segmental distribution, 2.8 cm from the nipple. This likely
   represents a second site of disease within the left breast — multicentric versus
   multifocal disease cannot be excluded without histologic sampling.
3. Two morphologically abnormal left axillary lymph nodes, suspicious for metastatic
   involvement.
4. No contralateral breast abnormality.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 6 (primary lesion; known ultrasound BI-RADS 5) / 4B (secondary NME
lesion — suspicious, biopsy recommended)

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the dominant left breast mass.
Consider MRI-guided biopsy of the secondary NME at 9 o'clock left breast if primary
biopsy is confirmed malignant and if histologic characterization of the secondary lesion
would alter surgical planning. Surgical oncology consultation recommended.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin, labeled "left breast core biopsy," are six tan-white cylindrical
core fragments ranging from 1.2 to 1.7 cm in length. All cores are submitted entirely
in two cassettes (A1–A2).

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show cores of fibroadipose breast tissue with a malignant epithelial
proliferation. The tumor is composed of sheets and nests of highly pleomorphic cells
with vesicular nuclei, prominent nucleoli, and a moderate amount of eosinophilic
cytoplasm. Mitotic figures are numerous (>20 per 10 high-power fields), including
atypical forms. There is central comedo-type necrosis within nests. Stromal desmoplastic
reaction is prominent. No in situ component is identified on the sampled cores.
No lymphovascular invasion is identified in the cores; however, this assessment
is limited given the biopsy nature of the specimen.

Invasive ductal carcinoma (invasive carcinoma of no special type, NST), left breast,
10 o'clock position, measuring at least 3.8 cm by imaging correlation.
Nuclear grade: 3 (high grade).
Nottingham histologic grade: 3/3 (tubule formation: 3; nuclear pleomorphism: 3;
mitotic count: 3). Elston-Ellis Grade 3.
