BIOPSY PROCEDURE NOTE / PATHOLOGY REPORT

Procedure Date: 03/18/2024
Specimen Received: 03/18/2024
Report Issued: 03/21/2024

CLINICAL INFORMATION: 54-year-old female. Left breast mass, 10 o'clock, 4.5 cm from nipple. BI-RADS 4C on mammogram and ultrasound.

PROCEDURE: Ultrasound-guided 14-gauge core needle biopsy (CNB) of the left breast mass was performed using real-time ultrasound guidance. Five core specimens were obtained. A titanium biopsy clip (omega-shaped) was deployed at the biopsy site following specimen retrieval, confirmed by post-procedural mammographic image to be in satisfactory position within the 10 o'clock left breast mass. Hemostasis was achieved without complication.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin are five tan-white cylindrical core biopsy specimens, each measuring 0.8–1.2 cm in length and 0.1 cm in diameter. The cores are submitted entirely in one block, labeled "Left Breast CNB."

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show cores of fibrofatty breast tissue infiltrated by a moderately differentiated (Nottingham grade 2) invasive carcinoma. The tumor cells are arranged in glandular formations and solid nests with moderate nuclear pleomorphism. Nuclei are vesicular with prominent nucleoli. Mitotic figures are present (approximately 8 per 10 high-power fields). Scattered fine calcifications are identified within and adjacent to tumor nests. Associated high-grade ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS) with cribriform and solid architecture is present at the biopsy margins.

NOTTINGHAM HISTOLOGIC GRADE:
- Gland formation: Score 2
- Nuclear pleomorphism: Score 2
- Mitotic count: Score 2
- TOTAL: 6/9 — Grade 2 (Moderately Differentiated)

INVASIVE COMPONENT SIZE (core biopsy estimate): At least 1.8 cm (correlate with excision specimen for definitive sizing).

PATHOLOGIC DIAGNOSIS:
LEFT BREAST, 10 O'CLOCK, ULTRASOUND-GUIDED CORE NEEDLE BIOPSY:
- Invasive ductal carcinoma (IDC), Nottingham grade 2, measuring at least 1.8 cm
- Associated ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS), cribriform and solid type, high nuclear grade, with associated calcifications

COMMENT: Biopsy clip placement confirmed by post-procedure mammography. Correlation with clinical and radiologic findings is recommended. Surgical evaluation advised.
