STEREOTACTIC BIOPSY
Date of Procedure: 03/21/2024
Procedure: Left breast stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB), 9-gauge
Indication: BI-RADS 4B amorphous calcifications, left upper outer quadrant,
10 o'clock, middle depth, ~4.5 cm from nipple.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was positioned prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The left breast
was compressed and the target calcification cluster was identified stereotactically.
Local anesthesia was administered with 1% lidocaine with epinephrine. A 9-gauge
vacuum-assisted biopsy device (Hologic Eviva) was deployed via a lateral approach.
Multiple core specimens (12 cores) were obtained. Calcifications were confirmed
present within the specimen radiograph. Post-fire stereo pair images confirmed
adequate sampling of the target.

CLIP PLACEMENT:
A metallic biopsy marker clip (MicroMark II, oval-shaped) was deployed at the
biopsy site following specimen collection. Post-clip deployment scout image confirmed
satisfactory clip placement within the biopsy cavity in the left upper outer quadrant,
10 o'clock position.

Post-procedure mammographic images were obtained confirming clip position. The patient
tolerated the procedure well without complication. Specimen was submitted to pathology
in formalin-fixed containers labeled "Left breast stereotactic core biopsy —
10 o'clock, 4.5 cm FN."

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin, labeled as above, are 12 tan-white cylindrical core tissue
fragments, ranging from 0.8 to 1.4 cm in length. Representative sections entirely
submitted in cassettes A1–A4. Specimen radiograph confirmed presence of calcifications
in submitted cores.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show breast core tissue with ductal structures. There is intraductal
proliferation characterized by uniform, low-to-intermediate nuclear grade cells
arranged in cribriform and micropapillary patterns filling and distending the involved
ducts. Necrosis is focal and punctate (comedonecrosis, limited). The nuclear atypia
is intermediate grade with moderate nuclear pleomorphism, inconspicuous nucleoli,
and a mitotic index of approximately 2–3 per 10 HPF.

Calcifications are identified within the lesional ducts, consistent with the
imaging-detected amorphous calcifications. The process appears localized to a
small region of tissue sampled.

No evidence of invasive carcinoma is identified within the submitted cores.
No lymphovascular invasion is seen.

DIAGNOSIS:
Left breast, 10 o'clock position, stereotactic vacuum-assisted core needle biopsy:
DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), INTERMEDIATE GRADE, cribriform and micropapillary
patterns, with focal punctate comedonecrosis; spanning approximately 1.8 cm by
imaging correlation. Calcifications present.
