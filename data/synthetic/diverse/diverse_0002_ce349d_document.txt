TARGET LESION:

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was placed in the supine oblique position. The target lesion was identified
in real-time ultrasound. The skin was prepped in sterile fashion. After adequate local
anesthesia, a co-axial introducer was placed. Using a 14-gauge automated spring-loaded

in formalin and submitted to surgical pathology.

Clip Placement: A titanium biopsy marker clip (diamond-shaped) was deployed within the
lesion under direct ultrasound visualization at the 10 o'clock position following
specimen acquisition. Post-deployment mammogram confirmed satisfactory clip placement
within the right breast biopsy site.

Complications: None. Hemostasis achieved with manual compression.
