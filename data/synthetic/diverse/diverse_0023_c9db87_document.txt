ULTRASOUND — LEFT BREAST AND AXILLA
Date of Examination: 03/11/2024
Performing Technologist: M. Hartley, RDMS
Interpreting Radiologist: Dr. L. Vásquez, MD

CLINICAL INDICATION:
Patient is a 52-year-old female presenting with a palpable left breast
mass at the 10 o'clock position, noted by the patient for approximately
six weeks. No prior breast imaging on file at this institution.

TECHNIQUE:
High-resolution grayscale and color Doppler ultrasound of the left
breast and left axilla was performed using a 15 MHz linear-array
transducer.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:

LESION 1 (INDEX / PRIMARY):
A hypoechoic, irregular mass with angular and spiculated margins was
identified at the 10 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant of the
left breast, approximately 3.0 cm from the nipple, at mid depth.
Dimensions: 2.8 x 2.1 x 2.4 cm. Internal vascularity was increased
on Doppler interrogation. No posterior acoustic enhancement.
Posterior acoustic shadowing was present.

LESION 2 (SATELLITE):
A second, smaller hypoechoic irregular mass was identified at the
10-11 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant, left breast,
approximately 1.5 cm anterior (superficial) to the index lesion,
within the same quadrant. Dimensions: 0.9 x 0.7 x 0.8 cm.
Similar morphologic features to Lesion 1.

CALCIFICATIONS:
Punctate echogenic foci suggestive of associated microcalcifications
were identified within and immediately adjacent to the index mass
(Lesion 1). Morphology and distribution could not be fully
characterized by ultrasound; mammographic and/or MRI correlation
was recommended.

LEFT AXILLA:
An abnormal lymph node was identified in the left axilla at Level I.
The node measured 2.2 x 1.4 cm with cortical thickening (cortex
measuring 5 mm, markedly asymmetric), loss of fatty hilum, and
increased peripheral vascularity. Findings are suspicious for
metastatic involvement.

RIGHT BREAST AND AXILLA: Not examined at this encounter.

IMPRESSION:
1. Left breast, 10 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant: Irregular
   hypoechoic mass measuring 2.8 cm (greatest dimension), suspicious
   for malignancy — BI-RADS 5.
2. Left breast, 10-11 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant: Second
   irregular hypoechoic mass measuring 0.9 cm, suspicious for
   malignancy — consistent with multifocal disease — BI-RADS 5.
3. Left axilla Level I: Abnormal lymph node morphology suspicious for
   metastatic involvement.

RECOMMENDATION:
Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast index lesion
(Lesion 1) and the satellite lesion (Lesion 2) is recommended.
Ultrasound-guided fine needle aspiration or core needle biopsy of the
suspicious left axillary lymph node should be performed concurrently.
MRI of the bilateral breasts is recommended for extent-of-disease
evaluation prior to surgical planning.

TECHNIQUE:
Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla scanner (Siemens
Prisma) using a dedicated 16-channel bilateral breast coil. Sequences
included axial T1-weighted, T2-weighted fat-saturated, diffusion-
weighted imaging (DWI), and dynamic contrast-enhanced (DCE) series
following intravenous administration of gadobutrol 0.1 mmol/kg.
Subtraction images and kinetic curve analysis were performed.

FINDINGS:

BACKGROUND PARENCHYMAL ENHANCEMENT (BPE): Mild, symmetric.

LEFT BREAST:

LESION 1 (INDEX MASS):
An irregularly shaped, heterogeneously enhancing mass was identified
at the 10 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant, left breast, at mid
depth, 3.0 cm from the nipple. Mass dimensions on MRI: 3.1 x 2.5 x
2.6 cm. Kinetic curve analysis demonstrated type III (washout) curve
morphology, highly suspicious for malignancy. Rim enhancement pattern
was noted at the periphery. Associated restricted diffusion on DWI
(ADC value approximately 0.82 x 10⁻³ mm²/s).

LESION 2 (SATELLITE MASS):
A second enhancing irregular mass was identified at the 10-11 o'clock
position, upper outer quadrant, left breast, superficial depth,
approximately 1.8 cm anterior to the index lesion. Dimensions on MRI:
1.0 x 0.8 x 0.9 cm. Type III kinetic curve. Findings are consistent
with multifocal disease within the same quadrant.

NON-MASS ENHANCEMENT (NME):
Segmental non-mass enhancement was identified extending from the
index lesion posteriorly toward the nipple along the 10 o'clock
distribution, spanning approximately 4.5 cm. Enhancement pattern was
clumped. This finding is suspicious and may represent an associated
DCIS component extending beyond the invasive masses.

CALCIFICATIONS ON MRI:
Faint susceptibility artifacts correlating to known microcalcifications
within the index lesion were noted on susceptibility-weighted sequences.

LEFT AXILLA:
Morphologically abnormal lymph node identified in Level I left axilla,
corresponding to the node noted on ultrasound of 03/11/2024. Dimensions
2.3 x 1.5 cm. Cortical thickening and loss of fatty hilum confirmed.
Enhancing with contrast administration. Findings are consistent with
suspected nodal metastasis.

RIGHT BREAST:
No suspicious mass, architectural distortion, or abnormal enhancement.
Right axilla: No morphologically abnormal lymph nodes identified.

IMPRESSION:
1. Left breast 10 o'clock, upper outer quadrant: Irregularly enhancing
   mass (3.1 cm greatest dimension on MRI), type III washout kinetics,
   highly suspicious for malignancy — BI-RADS 6 (biopsy already planned).
2. Second satellite enhancing mass (1.0 cm) at 10-11 o'clock, upper

3. Segmental non-mass enhancement (4.5 cm span) along the 10 o'clock
   distribution — suspicious; may represent extensive DCIS component.
4. Left Level I axillary lymph node with morphologic features suspicious
   for metastatic involvement.
5. Right breast: No suspicious findings — BI-RADS 1.

RECOMMENDATION:
Proceed with ultrasound-guided biopsy of the left breast lesions and
left axillary lymph node as recommended. Surgical oncology and
multidisciplinary tumor board evaluation recommended following
pathologic confirmation.

CLINICAL INDICATION:
Left breast multifocal suspicious masses and abnormal left axillary
lymph node as characterized on ultrasound (03/11/2024) and MRI
(03/15/2024). Biopsy for histologic diagnosis.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:

LESION 1 — INDEX MASS:
Under continuous real-time ultrasound guidance, the skin over the left
breast at the 10 o'clock position was prepped and draped in the usual
sterile fashion. Local anesthesia was achieved using 1% lidocaine
without epinephrine. A 14-gauge automated spring-loaded core needle
was introduced and advanced to the target mass at 10 o'clock, upper
outer quadrant, left breast, mid depth, 3.0 cm from the nipple.
Five (5) core tissue samples were obtained with good representative
tissue confirmed by the performing radiologist. Specimens were placed
in formalin for processing.

CLIP PLACEMENT — LESION 1:
A titanium ribbon-shaped biopsy marker clip was deployed under
ultrasound visualization at the posterior margin of the index mass,
10 o'clock position, left breast. Post-deployment images confirmed
satisfactory clip position within the lesion.

LESION 2 — SATELLITE MASS:
The satellite mass at the 10-11 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant,
left breast (superficial depth) was targeted using an identical
technique. Four (4) core tissue samples were obtained using a 14-gauge
automated core needle. Representative tissue confirmed.

CLIP PLACEMENT — LESION 2:
A second titanium ribbon-shaped biopsy marker clip was placed within
the satellite lesion at 10-11 o'clock, left breast. Clip position
confirmed on post-deployment ultrasound images.

LEFT AXILLARY LYMPH NODE:
Under ultrasound guidance, the morphologically abnormal Level I left
axillary lymph node was targeted. Using a 22-gauge needle, fine needle
aspiration (FNA) was performed with three (3) passes. Material was
expressed onto slides and submitted for cytologic evaluation.

POST-PROCEDURE:
Hemostasis achieved with manual compression. Patient tolerated the
procedure well without immediate complications.

RECOMMENDATION:
Correlate biopsy results with imaging. Surgical oncology referral
recommended upon pathologic confirmation. Multidisciplinary tumor board
review advised.
