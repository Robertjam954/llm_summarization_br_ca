stromal invasion is identified on routine hematoxylin and eosin-stained sections
despite extensive sampling. No invasive carcinoma is present in the submitted
material. Periductal chronic inflammatory infiltrate and fibrosis are noted. No
lymphovascular invasion is identified.

— DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), HIGH-GRADE, COMEDO TYPE, WITH EXTENSIVE
  CALCIFICATIONS.
— NO INVASIVE CARCINOMA IDENTIFIED.
— PATHOLOGIC EXTENT: Involving 8 of 12 cores. Given the imaging span of 5.4 cm
