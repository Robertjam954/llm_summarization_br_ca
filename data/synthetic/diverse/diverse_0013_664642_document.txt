STEREOTACTIC BIOPSY — PROCEDURE NOTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Date of Procedure: 03/19/2024
Procedure: Stereotactic vacuum-assisted core needle biopsy (VAB),
right breast calcifications.
Operator: Dr. L. Brennan, MD
Needle/System: 9-gauge vacuum-assisted needle (Hologic Eviva system),
stereotactic guidance.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was placed in prone position on the stereotactic biopsy table.
The right breast was compressed and the target calcification cluster at the
11 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant, was identified on stereo pair
images. Following administration of local anesthesia (1% lidocaine with
epinephrine), a 9-gauge vacuum-assisted needle was advanced to the target
site under stereotactic guidance. A total of twelve core specimens were
obtained. Post-fire stereo images confirmed adequate sampling of the
calcification cluster.

A titanium biopsy marker clip (MicroMark II, helical coil configuration)
was deployed at the biopsy site following specimen retrieval. Post-deployment
specimen radiograph demonstrated calcifications within multiple retrieved
cores. Post-biopsy mammographic images confirmed appropriate clip placement
within the region of calcifications at the 11 o'clock position, right
upper outer quadrant.

Specimens submitted in formalin for histopathologic analysis.

No immediate complications. Patient tolerated procedure well.

Procedure Radiologist: Dr. L. Brennan, MD
