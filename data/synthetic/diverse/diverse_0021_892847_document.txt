ULTRASOUND — LEFT BREAST
Date of Examination: 02/14/2024
Accession: US-2024-04418

CLINICAL INDICATION: Palpable left breast mass, self-detected approximately three weeks
prior. No prior breast imaging on file at this institution.

TECHNIQUE: Real-time grayscale and Doppler ultrasound of the left breast and left
axilla was performed using a high-frequency linear transducer.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST: A large, irregular, hypoechoic mass was identified in the left breast at
the 9 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant, approximately 4.0 cm from the nipple,
at a mid-depth level within the breast parenchyma. The mass measured 3.8 x 3.1 x
3.4 cm in three orthogonal dimensions. Margins were markedly irregular and spiculated.
There was posterior acoustic shadowing. Internal vascularity was increased on Doppler
interrogation. No discrete satellite nodules were identified in the immediate vicinity.
No calcifications were sonographically apparent.

LEFT AXILLA: There were two left axillary lymph nodes with eccentric cortical thickening
measuring up to 7 mm, suspicious for metastatic involvement. Loss of fatty hilum
was noted in the more anterior node.

RIGHT BREAST AND RIGHT AXILLA: Not evaluated at this time.

IMPRESSION:
1. Large irregular hypoechoic mass, left breast, 9 o'clock position, 4.0 cm from the
   nipple, measuring 3.8 x 3.4 x 3.1 cm. Findings are highly suspicious for malignancy.
   BI-RADS 5.
2. Suspicious left axillary lymphadenopathy with cortical thickening and loss of
   fatty hilum, concerning for nodal metastatic disease.

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast mass is
recommended. Biopsy of the suspicious left axillary lymph node should also be considered.
MRI of the bilateral breasts is recommended for extent-of-disease evaluation prior to
definitive surgical planning.

MRI — BILATERAL BREASTS
Date of Examination: 02/22/2024
Accession: MRI-2024-00873

CLINICAL INDICATION: Known left breast mass, BI-RADS 5 on recent ultrasound. Pre-biopsy
extent-of-disease evaluation.

TECHNIQUE: Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla system using a dedicated
breast coil. Sequences included axial T1-weighted pre- and post-contrast, T2-weighted
fat-suppressed, and diffusion-weighted imaging. Gadolinium-based contrast was
administered intravenously. Dynamic contrast-enhanced subtraction images were
obtained and kinetic curve analysis was performed.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST: There is a large irregular enhancing mass in the left breast at the
9 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant, mid depth, approximately 4.0 cm from the
nipple. The mass demonstrates avid, heterogeneous enhancement with a type III
(washout) kinetic curve pattern. Maximum dimensions on MRI measure 4.2 x 3.5 x 3.6 cm.
Margins are irregular and spiculated. On DWI, the mass demonstrates restricted
diffusion with an ADC value of approximately 0.78 x 10⁻³ mm²/s, consistent with high
cellularity.

ADDITIONAL ENHANCEMENT: There is a focus of non-mass enhancement (NME) in the left
breast at the 10 o'clock position, approximately 2.5 cm from the nipple, measuring
approximately 0.9 cm in greatest dimension, with clumped internal enhancement. This
focus is separate from the index mass and may represent a satellite focus of disease
or adenosis; correlation with second-look ultrasound is recommended.

There is thickening and abnormal enhancement of the overlying skin at the 9 o'clock
position, which may reflect early dermal involvement or lymphedema.

RIGHT BREAST: No suspicious enhancing mass or non-mass enhancement. No architectural
distortion. Scattered fibroglandular tissue. Background parenchymal enhancement is
minimal.

LYMPH NODES: Two left axillary lymph nodes demonstrate abnormal morphology with
eccentric cortical thickening (up to 8 mm) and absent fatty hilum, concordant with
ultrasound findings. No right axillary adenopathy.

IMPRESSION:
1. Large irregular enhancing mass, left breast, 9 o'clock, upper outer quadrant,
   mid depth, 4.0 cm from nipple, measuring 4.2 x 3.5 x 3.6 cm on MRI. Type III
   washout kinetic curve. Highly suspicious for malignancy. BI-RADS 5.
2. Additional focus of non-mass enhancement, left breast, 10 o'clock, 2.5 cm from
   the nipple, 0.9 cm, with clumped enhancement — BI-RADS 4B. Cannot exclude
   satellite malignancy. Second-look ultrasound recommended.
3. Suspicious left axillary lymphadenopathy, concordant with ultrasound.
4. Disease appears localized to the left breast with ipsilateral nodal involvement.
   No contralateral disease identified.

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast index mass.
Second-look ultrasound and possible biopsy of the 10 o'clock focus. Multidisciplinary
tumor board discussion suggested for surgical planning.

ULTRASOUND-GUIDED CORE NEEDLE BIOPSY — LEFT BREAST
Date of Procedure: 02/28/2024
Accession: BX-2024-00521
Performing Physician: S. Hernandez, MD

INDICATION: Left breast mass, BI-RADS 5, 9 o'clock position, 4.0 cm from nipple.

PROCEDURE NOTE:
Informed consent was obtained. The patient was positioned supine with the left arm
raised. The left breast was prepped and draped in a sterile fashion. The index mass
at the 9 o'clock position was localized under real-time ultrasound guidance. Local
anesthesia was achieved with 1% lidocaine. Using a co-axial technique, a 14-gauge
automated core biopsy needle was advanced into the mass under continuous ultrasound
visualization. Six core biopsy samples were obtained from representative areas of the
lesion. The cores were tan-white and appeared adequate.

CLIP PLACEMENT: No biopsy clip or marker was placed at the conclusion of the procedure
given the large size of the mass and clinical context.

POST-PROCEDURE: Hemostasis was achieved with manual compression. Post-biopsy
ultrasound confirmed no hematoma.

SPECIMENS SUBMITTED: 6 core biopsy fragments, left breast, 9 o'clock mass, submitted
in formalin to Surgical Pathology.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show cores of breast tissue extensively infiltrated by a high-grade invasive
carcinoma composed of poorly differentiated, pleomorphic cells arranged in nests,

Stromal desmoplasia is present. Lymphovascular space invasion is identified focally.
No in situ carcinoma component is identified in these core biopsy samples.
