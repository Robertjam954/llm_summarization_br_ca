[PATIENT] is a 54-year-old female presenting for diagnostic mammographic evaluation following recall from screening mammogram performed 03/06/2024. Screening images demonstrated a cluster of calcifications in the left breast warranting additional characterization.

TECHNIQUE:
Standard two-view diagnostic mammography of the left breast was performed (CC and MLO), supplemented by magnification views in the CC and ML projections centered over the region of concern.

FINDINGS:
Magnification views of the left breast demonstrate a cluster of amorphous calcifications located in the upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position, approximately 5 cm from the nipple, in the anterior-to-middle depth of the breast. No suspicious calcifications are identified in the right breast. No axillary adenopathy is detected on the imaged portion of the left axilla.

IMPRESSION:
Left breast, upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock position, 5 cm from the nipple: Grouped amorphous calcifications spanning 1.8 cm.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 4B — Suspicious. Tissue sampling recommended.

Stereotactic vacuum-assisted core needle biopsy of the left breast calcifications is recommended. Correlation with pathology results advised.

STEREOTACTIC VACUUM-ASSISTED BIOPSY — LEFT BREAST
Date of Procedure: 03/21/2024
Performing Radiologist: Dr. A. Petrakis, MD
Assisting Technologist: R. Holloway, RT(R)(M)

PROCEDURE NOTE:
Informed consent was obtained from [PATIENT] prior to the procedure. The left breast was positioned in the stereotactic biopsy unit in the prone position. Stereo pair images were obtained confirming localization of the target cluster of amorphous calcifications at the 10 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant, approximately 5 cm from the nipple.

Following sterile preparation and local anesthesia with 1% lidocaine with epinephrine, a 9-gauge vacuum-assisted biopsy needle (Mammotome, Hologic) was introduced via a lateral approach. Stereotactic pre-fire and post-fire images confirmed accurate needle placement within the target calcification cluster.

A total of twelve (12) core specimens were obtained. Specimen radiograph confirmed the presence of calcifications within the retrieved tissue cores. The cores were submitted in formalin for pathologic evaluation.

CLIP PLACEMENT:
Following specimen retrieval, a titanium biopsy clip marker (oval/elliptical configuration) was deployed at the biopsy site. Post-deployment mammographic images in two projections confirmed satisfactory clip placement within the biopsy cavity corresponding to the 10 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant of the left breast, 5 cm from the nipple.

COMPLICATIONS: None.

Post-procedure instructions were provided to the patient. Pathology follow-up recommended.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin, labeled "left breast stereotactic biopsy cores," are twelve (12) tan-white cylindrical core tissue fragments ranging from 0.9 to 1.4 cm in length. The specimen is entirely submitted in six cassettes.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show fibroadipose and fibroglandular breast tissue. Within the ductal structures, there is a proliferation of intermediate-grade ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS). The involved ducts demonstrate a predominantly solid and cribriform growth pattern. Nuclear grade is intermediate (grade 2), with moderate nuclear pleomorphism, inconspicuous-to-occasional nucleoli, and moderate mitotic activity. Necrosis is focal and comedo-type necrosis is not a prominent feature.

Calcifications of the amorphous type are identified within and adjacent to the involved ducts, consistent with the imaging findings. The DCIS appears localized in distribution, involving a contiguous region estimated at approximately 1.5 cm based on the involved core distribution.

No invasive carcinoma is identified in the material submitted. No lymphovascular invasion is seen. Surrounding breast parenchyma shows fibrocystic changes including mild stromal fibrosis and columnar cell change.

PATHOLOGIC DIAGNOSIS:
Left breast, upper outer quadrant, stereotactic core biopsy:
DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), INTERMEDIATE GRADE (nuclear grade 2), solid and cribriform pattern, with associated amorphous-type calcifications. Estimated extent: approximately 1.5 cm. No invasive carcinoma identified.
