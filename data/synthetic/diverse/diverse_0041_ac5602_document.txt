CLINICAL INDICATION: 54-year-old female presenting with a palpable
mass in the left breast at the 10 o'clock position. Family history
significant for maternal breast cancer.

TECHNIQUE: High-resolution grayscale and color Doppler ultrasound
of the left breast and left axilla was performed using a 12–5 MHz
linear transducer.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
Two distinct solid hypoechoic masses were identified in the left breast.

Mass 1 (Index Lesion):
A 2.8 cm irregular, hypoechoic mass with angular margins and posterior
acoustic shadowing was identified at the 10 o'clock position, middle
depth, approximately 3.5 cm from the left nipple. Internal vascularity
was increased on color Doppler interrogation.

Mass 2 (Secondary Lesion):
A 1.2 cm irregular hypoechoic mass with indistinct margins was identified
at the 9 o'clock position, middle depth, approximately 4.8 cm from the
left nipple. This lesion was considered separate from the index lesion.

Skin thickening was noted overlying the 10 o'clock region. No skin
retraction was identified.

LEFT AXILLA:
An enlarged left axillary lymph node measuring 2.1 cm in long axis was
identified with markedly thickened cortex (cortical thickness 0.9 cm)
and loss of fatty hilum, highly suspicious for metastatic involvement.

IMPRESSION:
1. Two suspicious solid masses in the left breast at 10 o'clock and
   9 o'clock positions, consistent with multifocal disease. BI-RADS 5.
2. Left axillary lymphadenopathy with morphologic features suspicious for
   metastatic involvement.
3. Ultrasound-guided biopsy of the dominant left breast mass and left
   axillary lymph node is recommended. Correlation with MRI recommended
   for further extent-of-disease evaluation.

CLINICAL INDICATION: Known left breast masses on ultrasound, BI-RADS 5.
Extent-of-disease evaluation prior to surgical planning.

TECHNIQUE: Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla scanner
(Siemens Vida) using a dedicated 16-channel breast coil. Sequences
included T1-weighted, T2-weighted fat-saturated, and dynamic contrast-
enhanced (DCE) series following intravenous administration of 0.1 mmol/kg
gadobutrol (Gadavist). Subtraction images and kinetic curve analysis
were generated. Diffusion-weighted imaging was also performed.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
Mass Enhancement — Index Lesion:
An irregular mass with spiculated margins demonstrating avid heterogeneous
enhancement was identified at the 10 o'clock position, middle depth,
3.5 cm from the nipple. The lesion measured 3.1 cm in greatest dimension
on post-contrast T1 subtraction images. Kinetic curve analysis demonstrated
washout enhancement kinetics (type III curve), highly suspicious for
malignancy.

Mass Enhancement — Secondary Lesion:
A second enhancing mass measuring 1.3 cm in greatest dimension was
identified at the 9 o'clock position, middle depth, 4.8 cm from the
nipple, corresponding to the second lesion seen on ultrasound. This
lesion also demonstrated washout kinetics.

Non-Mass Enhancement:
Additional segmental non-mass enhancement (NME) was identified in the
upper outer quadrant of the left breast extending approximately 5.5 cm in
craniocaudal dimension. The distribution and clumped internal enhancement
pattern are suspicious and may represent an extensive intraductal
component or associated DCIS extending beyond the masses.

RIGHT BREAST: No suspicious enhancement. No mass lesions. No abnormal
lymph nodes.

LEFT AXILLA: At least two enlarged left axillary lymph nodes were
identified with cortical thickening and loss of normal fatty hilum,
consistent with the ultrasound findings. The dominant node measured
2.2 cm.

IMPRESSION:
1. Multifocal left breast malignancy: index lesion at 10 o'clock
   (3.1 cm) and secondary lesion at 9 o'clock (1.3 cm), both with
   type III washout kinetics. BI-RADS 6 (known biopsy- proven
   malignancy, per clinical history — see addendum below).
2. Extensive segmental non-mass enhancement in the left upper outer
   quadrant (5.5 cm craniocaudal span), suspicious for associated DCIS.
3. Left axillary lymphadenopathy, consistent with nodal metastasis.
4. No contralateral disease.
5. Surgical oncology referral recommended for multidisciplinary
   treatment planning.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:

Site 1 — Left Breast Index Lesion (10 o'clock, 3.5 cm from nipple):
Informed consent was obtained. The patient was placed in the supine
oblique position. The index lesion at the 10 o'clock position was
identified under real-time ultrasound guidance. The skin and subcutaneous
tissues were anesthetized with 1% lidocaine. Using a coaxial system,
four cores were obtained with a 14-gauge automated spring-loaded core

clip (HydroMARK, ribbon configuration) was deployed at the biopsy site
under real-time ultrasound visualization. Post-deployment image confirmed
satisfactory clip placement within the lesion.

Site 2 — Left Axillary Lymph Node:
The dominant morphologically abnormal left axillary lymph node was
identified under ultrasound guidance. Two passes were made with a 22-gauge
fine needle for cytologic sampling (fine needle aspiration, FNA). Aspirate
material was placed on cytology slides and in CytoLyt solution.

Specimen radiograph of the breast cores confirmed presence of biopsy
clip artifact. Cores were submitted to surgical pathology in formalin.
Cytology samples submitted to cytopathology.

Post-biopsy instructions provided. No immediate complications.
