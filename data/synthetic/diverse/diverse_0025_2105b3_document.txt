STEREOTACTIC BIOPSY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Procedure Date: 03/22/2024
Procedure: Left breast stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB)

CLINICAL INDICATION:
Grouped amorphous calcifications in the left breast upper outer quadrant identified
on diagnostic mammography 03/14/2024. BI-RADS 4B. Proceeding with stereotactic
vacuum-assisted biopsy for tissue diagnosis.

TECHNIQUE:
The patient was positioned prone on the stereotactic biopsy table. The left breast was
compressed and digital stereotactic images were obtained to localize the target
calcification cluster at the 10 o'clock position, upper outer quadrant, approximately
3.5 cm from the nipple. Following standard skin preparation and administration of local
anesthesia with 1% lidocaine with epinephrine, an 11-gauge Mammotome vacuum-assisted
biopsy device was advanced to the target site using stereotactic guidance. Pre-fire
and post-fire confirmation images confirmed accurate needle placement within the
calcification cluster.

A total of twelve (12) cores were obtained. Specimen radiograph was performed and
confirmed retrieval of representative calcifications in multiple cores.

CLIP PLACEMENT:
A titanium biopsy marker clip (MicroMark II, oval shape) was deployed at the biopsy
site following tissue sampling. Post-deployment stereotactic images confirmed the clip
to be in satisfactory position within the left breast upper outer quadrant at the
10 o'clock position, consistent with the sampled calcification cluster.

Post-procedure mammogram (CC and MLO views) was obtained to document clip location.
No hematoma or pneumothorax was identified. Patient tolerated the procedure without
complication.

IMPRESSION:
Technically successful left breast stereotactic 11-gauge vacuum-assisted biopsy of
grouped amorphous calcifications, upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock, 3.5 cm from nipple.
Biopsy marker clip deployed and confirmed in satisfactory position.

Specimen submitted to surgical pathology.
