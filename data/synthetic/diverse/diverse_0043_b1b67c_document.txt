ULTRASOUND-GUIDED CORE NEEDLE BIOPSY — PROCEDURE NOTE
Procedure Date: 03/17/2024
Performing Radiologist: Dr. S. Patel, MD

INDICATION: Right breast mass, 10 o'clock, BI-RADS 5. Suspicious right low axillary lymph node.

PROCEDURE:
Informed consent was obtained. The patient was placed in the supine oblique position. The right
breast was prepped and draped in sterile fashion. Local anesthesia was administered with 1%
lidocaine. Under continuous real-time ultrasound guidance, a 14-gauge automated spring-loaded core
needle biopsy device was advanced in a freehand technique into the right breast mass at the
10 o'clock position, 4.5 cm from the nipple. Five (5) core specimens were obtained and submitted in
formalin for histologic analysis.

Following specimen acquisition, a metallic biopsy clip (BB-shaped titanium marker) was deployed at
the biopsy site under ultrasound visualization. Post-deployment mammographic images (CC and MLO)
confirmed the clip to be in satisfactory position within the right breast mass at the 10 o'clock
location. Clip placement was considered accurate.

Additionally, under separate ultrasound guidance, the right low axillary lymph node was sampled using
a 25-gauge fine needle aspiration (FNA) technique. Material was submitted for cytologic evaluation.

Hemostasis achieved. No immediate complications. Post-biopsy mammogram images obtained.
