Date of Procedure: 03/26/2024
Modality: Ultrasound-Guided Core Needle Biopsy, Right Breast

OPERATOR: Dr. R. Tanaka, MD, Interventional Breast Radiologist
ASSISTANT: M. Guerrero, RT(R)(M)
ANESTHESIA: Local — 1% lidocaine without epinephrine

CLINICAL INDICATION:
Right breast hypoechoic oval mass, 10 o'clock, anterior depth, BI-RADS 4A. Consent
obtained. Risks and benefits discussed with patient.

TECHNIQUE:
With the patient in the supine oblique position, the right breast was prepped and
draped in the usual sterile fashion. Real-time ultrasound guidance was utilized
throughout the procedure. The target lesion at 10 o'clock, anterior depth, 3.5 cm
from the nipple was confirmed sonographically. Local anesthesia was administered.
Using a 14-gauge automated core needle device, four passes were made through the
lesion in a freehand, co-axial-assisted technique. Adequate cores were obtained and
placed in formalin for histologic processing.

Following tissue sampling, a titanium biopsy marker clip (oval shape) was deployed
within the biopsy cavity under real-time ultrasound visualization. Post-deployment
clip position was confirmed sonographically and by orthogonal mammographic views
(CC and lateral). The clip was noted to be in satisfactory position within the right
breast at 10 o'clock, anterior depth, co-localizing with the sampled lesion.

Hemostasis was achieved. The patient tolerated the procedure well without immediate
complication.

SPECIMEN: Four tan-pink core needle biopsy specimens, each approximately 1.0–1.2 cm
in length, submitted in formalin to Surgical Pathology.

POST-PROCEDURE IMPRESSION:
Successful US-guided 14-gauge core needle biopsy of right breast mass, 10 o'clock,
anterior depth, with satisfactory biopsy marker clip placement.
