upper outer quadrant, 10 o'clock position, mid depth. Post-deployment
mammographic images confirmed satisfactory clip position at the biopsy
site with no significant migration.

COMPLICATIONS: None.

Ultrasound-guided 14-gauge core needle biopsy of the right breast
10 o'clock lesion performed successfully. Ribbon-shaped metallic clip placed
at biopsy site. Pathology pending.
