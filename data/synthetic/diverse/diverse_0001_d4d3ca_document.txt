STEREOTACTIC BIOPSY
Date of Procedure: 03/19/2024
Procedure: Left Breast Stereotactic Vacuum-Assisted Biopsy (VAB)
Performing Radiologist: Dr. P. Nguyen, MD
Clinical Indication: Left breast amorphous calcifications, BI-RADS 4B.
================================================================================

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was placed in the prone position on the stereotactic biopsy table. The left breast was compressed and stereotactic targeting of the calcification cluster at the 10 o'clock position was performed. Local anesthesia was achieved with 1% lidocaine with epinephrine. A 9-gauge vacuum-assisted biopsy device (Hologic Eviva) was advanced to the target site under stereotactic guidance. Multiple cores (12 passes) were obtained from the calcification cluster. Gross inspection of core samples revealed calcifications within the submitted tissue. A titanium biopsy clip (QuickClip, oval shape) was deployed at the biopsy site upon completion of sampling. Post-biopsy stereotactic images confirmed accurate clip placement within the calcification cluster at the 10 o'clock position.

SPECIMENS:
Core biopsy specimens submitted to Surgical Pathology, labeled "Left breast stereotactic biopsy — 10 o'clock, 4 cm from nipple."

Specimen radiograph confirmed the presence of calcifications within the submitted cores.

Post-procedure mammogram confirmed deployment of the biopsy clip and residual calcifications at the target site. The patient tolerated the procedure without complication.

The aggregate tissue is submitted entirely in three cassettes. Specimen radiograph confirmed calcifications within submitted cores.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show breast ductal tissue with a proliferation of intermediate-grade ductal cells filling and expanding multiple ducts in a cribriform and solid pattern. The neoplastic cells demonstrate mild-to-moderate nuclear pleomorphism with dispersed chromatin and small nucleoli. Mitotic figures are infrequent. Calcifications (psammomatous and amorphous type) are identified within the involved ducts, corresponding to the mammographic finding. There is no evidence of stromal invasion identified in the submitted cores. The surrounding stroma shows mild periductal chronic inflammation. No lobular neoplasia is identified.
