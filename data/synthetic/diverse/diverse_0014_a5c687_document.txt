TECHNIQUE:
High-resolution gray-scale and Doppler ultrasound of the left breast was performed using a
linear array transducer (7–15 MHz). Targeted evaluation was performed of the palpable
abnormality at the left breast 9 o'clock position.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
At the 9 o'clock position, approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple, in the anterior-to-mid
depth, there is an irregular hypoechoic mass measuring 3.8 x 3.2 x 3.0 cm (AP x
transverse x craniocaudal). The mass demonstrates non-parallel orientation, angular
margins, and posterior acoustic shadowing. Internal vascularity is increased on color
Doppler interrogation. Overlying skin thickening is noted, measuring up to 4 mm. No
associated calcifications are identified sonographically.

LEFT AXILLA:
An enlarged left axillary lymph node is identified measuring 2.1 cm in short-axis
dimension, with cortical thickening up to 9 mm and effacement of the fatty hilum, highly
suspicious for nodal metastasis.

No additional suspicious masses were identified in the left breast on survey imaging.

RIGHT BREAST AND AXILLA:
Limited survey of the right breast and right axilla was performed and was unremarkable.
No suspicious masses or lymphadenopathy identified on the right.

NOTE: No biopsy clip/marker was placed at the time of this ultrasound examination.

IMPRESSION:
1. Irregular hypoechoic mass, left breast, 9 o'clock position, 4.5 cm from the nipple,
   measuring 3.8 cm in greatest dimension — highly suspicious for malignancy.
2. Morphologically abnormal left axillary lymph node — highly suspicious for nodal
   involvement.
3. BI-RADS 5: Highly suggestive of malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION:
Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast mass and left axillary lymph node
is strongly recommended. Breast MRI is recommended for extent-of-disease evaluation prior
to surgical planning.

PROCEDURE NOTE:

Informed consent was obtained. The patient was positioned supine with the left arm
abducted. The left breast and left axilla were prepped and draped in the usual sterile
fashion. Local anesthesia was administered using 1% lidocaine without epinephrine.

LEFT BREAST MASS:
Under real-time ultrasound guidance, a 14-gauge automated core biopsy needle was
advanced into the left breast mass at the 9 o'clock position, 4.5 cm from the nipple.
A total of 6 cores were obtained. Cores were tan-white and submitted in formalin for
histopathologic analysis.

NOTE: No biopsy clip/marker was deployed following the left breast biopsy at the
request of the clinical team, as the patient was being considered for immediate
surgical planning.

LEFT AXILLARY LYMPH NODE:
Under real-time ultrasound guidance, a 22-gauge fine needle was used for aspiration of
the morphologically abnormal left axillary lymph node. Material was submitted for
cytologic analysis.

POST-PROCEDURE:
Hemostasis achieved with manual compression. Patient tolerated the procedure without
immediate complication. Standard post-biopsy instructions provided.

MRI — LEFT BREAST (EXTENT OF DISEASE)
Date of Exam: 03/18/2024

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TECHNIQUE:
Bilateral breast MRI was performed on a 3.0 Tesla magnet using a dedicated breast coil.
Sequences included axial T1-weighted pre- and post-contrast series (Multihance,
0.1 mmol/kg IV), axial T2 fat-saturated, and diffusion-weighted imaging. Dynamic
contrast-enhanced (DCE) subtraction images and kinetic curve analysis were generated.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
There is an irregular enhancing mass at the 9 o'clock position of the left breast,
anterior-to-mid depth, approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple, measuring 3.9 x 3.4 x
3.1 cm (AP x transverse x craniocaudal) on post-contrast sequences. The mass
demonstrates heterogeneous internal enhancement with rim accentuation. Kinetic
analysis reveals rapid initial enhancement with washout kinetics (Type III curve),
consistent with malignancy. Associated skin thickening overlying the mass is confirmed.

No additional satellite lesions or separate enhancing masses are identified in the
left breast. There is no evidence of contralateral (right breast) enhancement
abnormality.

ADDITIONAL ENHANCEMENT:
Segmental non-mass enhancement (NME) is identified extending anteriorly from the
primary mass toward the nipple, spanning approximately 2.2 cm in craniocaudal
extent, within the lower-inner aspect of the 9 o'clock distribution. This
finding is suspicious and may represent ductal spread of tumor or associated
in situ component.

LEFT AXILLA:
Multiple enlarged left axillary lymph nodes are identified, the largest measuring
2.3 cm in short axis with loss of fatty hilum. Findings are consistent with
suspected nodal metastases.

RIGHT BREAST AND AXILLA:
No suspicious enhancing lesions identified in the right breast. Right axillary lymph
nodes appear normal in morphology.

IMPRESSION:
1. Irregular enhancing left breast mass, 9 o'clock position, anterior-to-mid depth,
   4.5 cm from nipple, measuring 3.9 cm in greatest dimension on MRI —
   consistent with known biopsy-proven malignancy.
2. Associated segmental non-mass enhancement extending anteriorly from the primary
   mass, approximately 2.2 cm extent — suspicious, may represent in situ component
   or ductal tumor spread.
3. Multiple morphologically abnormal left axillary lymph nodes — highly suspicious
   for metastatic nodal disease.
4. No contralateral breast abnormality identified.
5. Disease appears localized to the left breast and left axilla.
6. BI-RADS 6: Known biopsy-proven malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION:
Multidisciplinary tumor board review recommended. Surgical oncology and medical
oncology consultation advised for treatment planning.

Sections show cores of breast parenchyma extensively replaced by a malignant epithelial

singly, morphologically consistent with metastatic carcinoma. Cell block confirms
presence of malignant epithelioid cells.
