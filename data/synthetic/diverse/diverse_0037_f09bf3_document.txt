MAMMOGRAM — BILATERAL DIAGNOSTIC
─────────────────────────────────────────────

CLINICAL INDICATION: 58-year-old female presenting for diagnostic mammogram following
patient-reported right breast skin changes and nipple inversion of four weeks duration.
Personal history: no prior breast cancer. Family history: maternal aunt with breast
cancer diagnosed in her 60s.

TECHNIQUE: Standard bilateral CC and MLO views were obtained, with additional right
ML and right spot-compression magnification views over the upper outer quadrant.

BREAST COMPOSITION: Heterogeneously dense breast tissue (category C), which may
obscure small masses.

FINDINGS:

RIGHT BREAST: There is an extensive cluster of pleomorphic calcifications with
a regional distribution spanning the right breast upper outer quadrant at
approximately the 10 o'clock position, extending from roughly 3.0 cm to 7.5 cm
from the nipple. The calcifications are punctate to fine linear and branching
in morphology, with a total extent measuring approximately 6.5 cm in the longest
dimension on CC view. No discrete associated soft tissue mass is identified. No
architectural distortion is seen on the standard or spot-compression views. The
overlying skin and nipple-areolar complex appear unremarkable on mammographic
assessment; the clinically reported nipple inversion was not fully assessed
mammographically and requires clinical correlation.

LEFT BREAST: No suspicious masses, calcifications, or architectural distortion.
Scattered benign- appearing calcifications are noted bilaterally.

LYMPH NODES: No pathologically enlarged axillary lymph nodes are identified
bilaterally on the imaged portions of the axillae.

IMPRESSION:
Right breast — Regional cluster of pleomorphic, fine linear, and branching
calcifications in the upper outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position,
spanning approximately 6.5 cm. This morphology and distribution are highly
suspicious for malignancy.

BI-RADS CATEGORY: 5 — Highly Suggestive of Malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION: Breast MRI for extent-of-disease evaluation prior to biopsy,
followed by stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy of the right breast
calcifications. Surgical consultation recommended.

STEREOTACTIC VACUUM-ASSISTED BIOPSY REPORT
─────────────────────────────────────────────

DATE OF PROCEDURE: 03/28/2023
PROCEDURE PERFORMED: Right breast stereotactic vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB),
11-gauge
PERFORMING RADIOLOGIST: R. Okonkwo, MD
CLINICAL INDICATION: Right breast upper outer quadrant pleomorphic calcifications,
BI-RADS 5. Patient consented and positioned prone on the stereotactic table.

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was positioned prone on the digital stereotactic biopsy table. The
right breast was compressed and the target calcification cluster in the upper
outer quadrant (10 o'clock, mid-depth, approximately 5.5 cm from the nipple)
was localized using scout and stereo pair images. Local anesthesia was
administered with 1% lidocaine with epinephrine. An 11-gauge vacuum-assisted
biopsy needle (Mammotome) was advanced to the target. Twelve core specimens
were obtained. Specimen radiographs confirmed retrieval of representative
calcifications in multiple cores.

CLIP PLACEMENT: A titanium biopsy clip marker (Hydromark coil-shaped marker)
was deployed at the biopsy site within the right breast upper outer quadrant
at the 10 o'clock position following specimen retrieval. Post-clip deployment
stereotactic images confirmed satisfactory clip positioning within the sampled
calcification field. Post-procedure mammogram (CC and ML views) performed to
document clip location.

SPECIMENS: Twelve core specimens submitted in formalin, labeled "right breast
stereotactic biopsy." Specimen radiograph confirmed calcifications in 9 of 12
cores.

COMPLICATIONS: None. Patient tolerated the procedure well.

POST-PROCEDURE RECOMMENDATION: Pathology correlation. Surgical consultation
recommended upon tissue diagnosis.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received in formalin, labeled as above, are twelve core needle biopsy specimens
ranging from 0.7 to 1.6 cm in length and 0.1 to 0.2 cm in diameter. The
cores are tan-white to pink and firm. Specimen radiograph confirms calcifications
present within multiple fragments. All cores are entirely submitted in six cassettes.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show breast tissue with extensive ductal carcinoma in situ, high nuclear
grade, solid and cribriform architectural patterns, with central comedo-type
necrosis. The neoplastic cells are large with pleomorphic nuclei, prominent
nucleoli, and frequent mitotic figures. Dystrophic calcifications are identified
within the necrotic centers and within the lumina of the involved ducts, which
are numerous and extensively distributed throughout the submitted tissue.
No evidence of invasive carcinoma is identified in the submitted material.
No lymphovascular invasion is identified.
The background breast parenchyma shows usual ductal hyperplasia and fibrocystic
changes.

PATHOLOGIC DIAGNOSIS:
Right breast, stereotactic core biopsy, 10 o'clock, upper outer quadrant:
DUCTAL CARCINOMA IN SITU (DCIS), HIGH NUCLEAR GRADE, solid and cribriform
types with comedo necrosis and extensive associated calcifications.
NO INVASIVE CARCINOMA IDENTIFIED.

STAGING NOTE: Based on biopsy material only; pathologic staging deferred to
excision specimen.
