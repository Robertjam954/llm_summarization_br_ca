MAMMOGRAM
Date of Examination: 03/04/2024
Facility: Mercy Regional Breast Center, Diagnostic Mammography Suite

CLINICAL INDICATION: 52-year-old female presenting for diagnostic mammogram following palpable
abnormality noted by patient in the right breast. Personal history: negative. Family history:
maternal aunt with postmenopausal breast cancer.

TECHNIQUE: Digital diagnostic bilateral mammography was performed including standard craniocaudal
(CC) and mediolateral oblique (MLO) views with additional spot compression views of the right
upper outer quadrant.

FINDINGS:

Right Breast: There is a small, well-circumscribed oval mass identified in the right breast upper
outer quadrant at the 10 o'clock position, approximately 4.5 cm from the nipple, in the anterior-
to- mid depth of the breast parenchyma. The mass measures approximately 0.8 cm in greatest
dimension on spot compression view. Margins appear partially obscured but predominantly
circumscribed. No associated suspicious calcifications are identified. No architectural distortion
is seen.

Left Breast: The breast parenchyma is heterogeneously dense (ACR category C). No suspicious mass,
calcification, or architectural distortion is identified.

Axillary Regions: No pathologically enlarged lymph nodes are identified bilaterally on the
available mammographic views.

IMPRESSION:
Right breast 10 o'clock position, anterior-to-mid depth, 4.5 cm from nipple: 0.8 cm oval mass
with partially obscured margins. No suspicious calcifications. No architectural distortion.

BI-RADS ASSESSMENT: 4A — Low suspicion for malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided evaluation of the right breast mass is recommended for
further characterization and tissue sampling.

CLINICAL INDICATION: Follow-up to diagnostic mammogram finding. Evaluation of right breast upper

and mild internal heterogeneity. No posterior acoustic shadowing. No internal vascularity on
color Doppler interrogation. No satellite lesions identified. The sonographic appearance is

US-GUIDED BIOPSY
Date of Procedure: 03/07/2024
Facility: Mercy Regional Breast Center, Interventional Suite
Performing Physician: James T. Hara, MD — Breast Imaging/Interventional

CLINICAL INDICATION: BI-RADS 4A right breast mass, 10 o'clock, 4.5 cm from nipple. Ultrasound-
guided core needle biopsy requested.

PROCEDURE: Informed consent was obtained. The right breast was prepped and draped in sterile
fashion. Real-time ultrasound guidance was used throughout the procedure. Local anesthesia was
achieved with 1% lidocaine without epinephrine. Using a 14-gauge automated spring-loaded core
biopsy needle, five (5) adequate core specimens were obtained from the right breast mass at the
10 o'clock position, 4.5 cm from the nipple. Cores were tan-white and submitted in formalin to
surgical pathology.

CLIP PLACEMENT: A titanium biopsy marker clip (ribbon configuration) was deployed within the
biopsy cavity under real-time ultrasound guidance immediately following specimen acquisition.
Post-deployment mammographic confirmation views (CC and MLO) were obtained and confirm
accurate clip placement within the right breast 10 o'clock mass. No clip migration was observed.

COMPLICATIONS: None. Hemostasis achieved. Patient tolerated procedure well.

SPECIMENS SUBMITTED: Right breast 10 o'clock core biopsy, 5 cores in formalin (labeled
"Right breast 10 o'clock core biopsy").

GROSS DESCRIPTION:

tissue fragments ranging from 0.8 to 1.4 cm in length. All cores are submitted in their entirety
in two (2) cassettes.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections reveal cores of fibrofatty breast tissue containing a focus of invasive carcinoma.
The carcinoma is composed of small, uniform glands and nests of low-grade epithelial cells with
round, regular nuclei, inconspicuous nucleoli, and scant mitotic activity. The growth pattern is
tubule-forming with greater than 75% tubule formation. Nuclear grade is 1 (low). Mitotic rate
is less than 1 per 10 high-power fields. No necrosis is identified. No lymphovascular invasion
is identified within the sampled cores.

Invasive carcinoma is present in 3 of 5 cores. The invasive carcinoma measures 0.7 cm in greatest
dimension on core biopsy material. Background breast tissue shows mild fibrocystic changes with
no evidence of in situ carcinoma in the sampled material.

HISTOLOGIC GRADE (Modified Scarff-Bloom-Richardson / Nottingham):
  Tubule formation: 1 (>75%)
  Nuclear pleomorphism: 1 (small, regular)
  Mitotic count: 1 (<1/10 HPF)
  Total Score: 3/9 — Grade 1 (Well differentiated)

DIAGNOSIS:
Right breast, 10 o'clock, core needle biopsy:
  INVASIVE DUCTAL CARCINOMA (Invasive Carcinoma of No Special Type), Nottingham Grade 1 (well-
  differentiated), measuring 0.7 cm in greatest dimension on core biopsy material.
  No lymphovascular invasion identified.
  No in situ component identified in sampled material.

NOTE: The biopsy marker clip artifact is noted radiographically. Correlation with imaging
is recommended. Definitive margin and size assessment require excision specimen.
