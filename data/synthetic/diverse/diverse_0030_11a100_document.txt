Indication: Palpable left breast mass. Personal history of atypical ductal hyperplasia.
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TECHNIQUE: High-resolution grayscale and Doppler ultrasonography of the left breast
and left axilla was performed using a 12–5 MHz linear array transducer.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
A dominant irregular hypoechoic mass was identified in the left breast at the
10 o'clock position, approximately 3.5 cm from the nipple, at mid-depth.
The mass measured 2.8 x 2.1 x 1.9 cm. Margins were spiculated with angular
features. Posterior acoustic shadowing was present. Internal vascularity was
demonstrated on color Doppler interrogation.

A second, smaller irregular hypoechoic focus was identified in the left breast
at the 9 o'clock position, approximately 4.0 cm from the nipple, also at mid-
depth, measuring 0.8 x 0.7 x 0.6 cm. This lesion demonstrated similar
morphologic features to the dominant mass.

No definite calcifications were identified on ultrasound; however, the spiculated
margins and lesion morphology are highly suspicious. No gross asymmetry was
assessed on this modality.

LEFT AXILLA:
An abnormal left axillary lymph node was identified measuring 2.2 x 1.4 cm
with cortical thickening (cortex measuring 0.9 cm), effacement of the fatty
hilum, and increased vascularity on Doppler. Findings are suspicious for
metastatic nodal involvement.

IMPRESSION:
1. Dominant left breast mass at 10 o'clock, 3.5 cm from nipple, measuring 2.8 cm —
   highly suspicious for malignancy.
2. Satellite lesion at 9 o'clock, 4.0 cm from nipple — also suspicious, raising
   concern for multifocal disease.
3. Abnormal left axillary lymph node — highly suspicious for metastatic involvement.
4. BI-RADS Category 5: Highly suggestive of malignancy.

RECOMMENDATION: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the dominant left breast
mass and the satellite lesion. Ultrasound-guided fine needle aspiration or core
biopsy of the suspicious left axillary lymph node. MRI of the bilateral breasts
recommended for extent-of-disease evaluation.

axial T1-weighted fat-suppressed pre- and post-contrast series, axial T2-weighted
fat-suppressed series, and diffusion-weighted imaging with ADC mapping.

FINDINGS:

LEFT BREAST:
DOMINANT MASS (L1): An irregularly marginated enhancing mass was identified at

component. Kinetic curve analysis demonstrated rapid initial enhancement with
washout kinetics (Type III curve), highly suspicious for malignancy. Restricted

9 o'clock position, mid-depth, 4.0 cm from the nipple, measuring 1.0 x 0.8 x

RIGHT BREAST: No suspicious mass, non-mass enhancement, or architectural distortion.

axillary lymph node, measuring up to 2.3 cm in greatest dimension.

SKIN / NIPPLE / CHEST WALL: No skin thickening, nipple retraction, or chest wall
involvement.

IMPRESSION:
1. Left breast dominant malignant-appearing mass at 10 o'clock, 3.5 cm from nipple
   — 3.1 cm on MRI.
2. Satellite malignant-appearing lesion at 9 o'clock — 1.0 cm on MRI. Together

3. Additional segmental non-mass enhancement, lower inner quadrant left breast —

4. Abnormal left axillary lymph node, highly suspicious for metastatic nodal disease.
5. BI-RADS Category 6 (left) / BI-RADS Category 1 (right).

RECOMMENDATION: Proceed with planned biopsy of dominant mass and satellite lesion.
