CLINICAL INDICATION: BI-RADS 4C mass, right breast
RADIOLOGIST: Dr. Michael K. Patterson, MD

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
Written informed consent was obtained. The patient was positioned supine. The right 
breast mass at 2 o'clock was identified with ultrasound guidance. After sterile prep 
and drape, local anesthesia was administered with 1% lidocaine. Under real-time 
ultrasound guidance, core needle biopsy was performed using a 14-gauge automated core 
biopsy device. Five core samples were obtained and submitted to pathology in 
formalin. A titanium clip marker was successfully deployed at the biopsy site under 
direct ultrasound visualization. Post-procedure mammographic images confirmed 
appropriate clip placement within the targeted lesion. Hemostasis was achieved and 
sterile dressing applied.

COMPLICATIONS: None

IMPRESSION:
Technically successful ultrasound-guided 14-gauge core needle biopsy of right breast 
mass at 2 o'clock with titanium clip placement.
